### 6. The learning ledger (`night_charge_ledger`, `recorder.ts`, v1.38.0)

One durable row per `plan_date` (`YYYY-MM-DD` in site time; primary key), in three column groups:

- **PLAN** (frozen the evening before; a re-issue for the same date overwrites only the plan columns): `issued_at_ms`, `algo_version` (stored TEXT — bumped only when a change leaves the earlier rows unable to judge the planner that runs now, which excludes them, §8; a physics refinement that keeps every scored column's meaning, such as v1.194.0's charge leg, keeps it), `posture` (the configured `NIGHT_CHARGE_MODE`: `advisory` / `supervised` / `auto`), `objective`, `rationale`, `confidence_tier`, `horizon_hours`, `soc_now_pct`, `soc_at_window_start_pct` (v1.39.0 — the plan's own projected pack SoC entering the window; NULL on every pre-v1.39.0 row), `target_soc_pct`, `buy_kwh`, `required_extra_kwh`, `reserve_floor_pct`, `cushion_pct`, `cushion_kwh`, `binding_cap`, the forecast basis (`pv_p10/p50/p90_kwh`, `load_p10/p50/p90_kwh`, `ev_p90_session_kwh`, `ev_session_count`, `band_sigma_cal`, `cal_scored_days`, `forecast_basis`, `weather_covered`), `min_proj_soc_pct`/`min_proj_soc_ts_ms` (the simulated plan trajectory — **omitted entirely for an incomplete-basis plan**, so a NULL here is the scorer's "no basis" sentinel), `pool_full_kwh`, `tariff_snapshot` (JSON: plan/season/period-now/ratesConfirmed/cents-by-tier), and **`window_start_ms` / `window_end_ms`** (v1.39.0) — the plan's *actually resolved* charge window. Weekend plans resolve windows disjoint from the canonical 23:00–05:00 night (a Saturday plan's window is Monday 00:00–05:00), and the scorer/completion gate must pair actuals to the **real** window; without these columns a Saturday night would be captured before its window even opened. Added to existing databases by an idempotent `ALTER TABLE` migration.
- **OUTCOME** (NULL until the night completes): `outcome_captured_at_ms`, `actual_pv_kwh`, `actual_load_kwh`, `actual_window_import_kwh`, `actual_onpeak_import_kwh`, `onpeak_import_occurred`, `actual_min_soc_pct`/`_ts_ms`, `plan_traj_floor_breached`, `cushion_breached`, `grid_home_coverage_frac`, `scored` (0/1), `score_notes`; v1.187.0: `onpeak_start_ms` / `onpeak_end_ms` / `onpeak_basis` and `realized_cost_cents` (both §7); v1.196.0: `window_close_soc_pct`, the pool measured at the window close (the median `backup_pct` over the window's last 15 minutes, `measuredWindowCloseSocPct`), which the realized cushion verdict reads (below).
- **ON-PEAK OUTCOME (v1.187.0).** `actual_onpeak_import_kwh` is `grid_home_w` import over the on-peak period the plan **governs** — the first run of on-peak hours at or after its own window closes, provided it starts before the next cheap window opens (`nightLedgerScoring.governedOnPeakSpan`, resolved from the tariff model, so the Mon–Fri gate and the holiday list are the tariff's own). A Mon–Thu plan governs the next day's 16:00–19:00; a Saturday or Sunday plan (window Monday 00:00–05:00) governs Monday's; a Friday plan governs none. `onpeak_basis` says which applies: `governed` (measured over `onpeak_start_ms`–`onpeak_end_ms`; NULL value when `grid_home_w` covers < 90% of the span), `none` (no on-peak governed — NULL, never a measured 0; also a plan that resolved no charge window at all, `windowlessLedgerColumns`), or `superseded` (a later plan resolved the same window — Sunday's for Saturday's — and carries it, so the shared Monday is recorded once). `onpeak_basis` NULL means **not measured on the governed basis**: a row captured before v1.187.0, or a pre-v1.39.0 row whose window was never recorded (nothing can be paired to it). **Rows captured before v1.187.0 carry `onpeak_basis` NULL and are kept as recorded:** their on-peak columns measured the plan day's own 16:00–19:00, which closes ~2.5 h before the ~21:30 plan exists, with no weekday gate — each weekday row holds the previous night's on-peak outcome, and Saturday/Sunday rows hold an off-peak afternoon. They are not rewritten.
- **PV EVIDENCE (v1.187.0).** `pv_model_sns` (plan time) records the Cores the PV band's solar model was fitted on (`ProbabilisticForecast.solarModelSns`, sorted, comma-joined). At capture, `pv_verdict_set_aside` records why the row's PV verdict is not forecast-skill evidence when that set differs from the Cores whose actual PV is summed (`pvVerdictSetAside`); `pv_in_band` / `pv_err_frac` are still written as measured. Rows planned before v1.187.0 have no `pv_model_sns` and their verdicts stand, with one exception in `KNOWN_FLEET_MISMATCH_PV_ROWS`, matched by exact `plan_date` + `issued_at_ms`: the 2026-09-27 plan, whose band came from the 21:06 restart's partial-map forecast (solar model fitted on one Core, v1.186.5) and was graded against three Cores' 9.6 kWh (`pv_in_band` 1). A once-per-boot pass (`tagKnownFleetMismatchPvRows`) writes only its `pv_verdict_set_aside`, logged; the captured verdict is kept.
- **THE CUSHION LINE'S CAPACITY BASIS.** `cushion_pool_scale` and `cushion_pool_scale_basis` (plan columns, `cushionPoolScaleLedgerColumns`, v1.199.0) record the measured capacity scale the line was converted on and its basis (`measured`, `held`, `min-known`, `nameplate`, or `implausible` where the scale passed was ignored), written on every plan row and NULL where no scale applied (a null plan; a `legacy-pct` or `disabled` cushion with no scale, a unit or an implausible one) and on rows written before them. Since v1.202.0 it is also the scale the plan's pool was counted on, so a `legacy-pct` or `disabled` row whose pool it moved carries it too.
- **THE PLAN'S POOL (v1.202.0).** `plan_pool_kwh` and `plan_walk_standby_w` (plan columns, `planPoolLedgerColumns`) record the pool the plan's walks counted (kWh per 100%: `pool_full_kwh` × `cushion_pool_scale`, chapter 15 part 1, "The plan on the packs' measured capacity") and the Cores' standby they drained beside the load (W). `pool_full_kwh` stays the panel's own figure. A sized row (a charge or a hold) carries both: its target, troughs and line are percents of `plan_pool_kwh`, its kWh figures are counted on it, and on the `legacy-pct` basis `cushion_kwh` is `cushion_pct` of it; where no scale moved the pool and no standby was drained they read `pool_full_kwh` and 0. A refused row (an incomplete basis, no window) counted no pool and reads NULL in both, as do rows written before v1.202.0, whose walks are on `pool_full_kwh` with no standby. Written unconditionally, so a re-issued plan replaces an earlier plan's pool.
- **THE CUSHION-ONLY PLAN (v1.203.0).** `cushion_only_refused_by` (plan column, `recordNightPlanRow`) is the solar basis gate's words a cushion-only plan was made on (chapter 15 part 1, "The cushion-only plan"); NULL on every other plan, a refusal included, and on earlier rows. The forecast columns stay the published band's (`pv_p10/50/90_kwh` 0 while the Cores are dark, the load figures, `cal_scored_days` 0), never what the plan used. - **THE SOLAR BASIS (v1.204.0).** Plan columns, written on every plan (NULL on earlier rows, so a re-issued plan never keeps an earlier one's): `pv_basis` (`cores` — the Cores' own band, covering every connected slot; `panel` / `mixed` — the panel's own per-slot readings; NULL — no solar basis: v1.203.0's plan (cushion-only, or its long-gap fill) or no plan), `pv_basis_slots` (each connected slot's source as of the plan's evening, `1:P,2:P,3:C`; slot numbers only), `pv_basis_refused_by` (why the panel's readings were not used, `code: words`; `transient` on the transient class; NULL when they were used or none was judged) and `pv_upper_ratio` (the 7-day energy balance of the window ending on the newest field day as of the plan's evening). Outcome column `actual_pv_basis`: the per-slot sources a panel row's PV actual was integrated from. Idempotent `ALTER`s; `CURRENT_ALGO_VERSION` stays 3. On a `panel` or `mixed` row `pv_model_sns` holds slot keys, and `pvVerdictSetAside` sets the PV verdict aside before any other rule (the row is kept apart from the readiness gate: chapter 15 part 2, "The write-readiness gate").
- **EXPECTED BUY (v1.187.22).** `expected_buy_kwh` and `expected_start_soc_pct` (plan columns, `expectedBuyLedgerColumns`) record the plan's expected buy and expected SoC at the window open (§5 "Expected buy"), beside `buy_kwh`, which keeps the worst case. `window_open_soc_pct` (outcome) is the pool SoC measured as the window opened — the median `backup_pct` over the 15 min before the open (`measuredWindowOpenSocPct`), NULL when nothing covers it. `score_notes` gains a clause grading them (`expectedBuyScoreNote`): the expected buy against `delivered_kwh` on an actuated night, and the expected window-open SoC against the measured one, with the islanded sizing start (`soc_at_window_start_pct`) beside it. Notes only: `buy_err_kwh`, the de-bias learner and the readiness gate do not read them. NULL on rows written before v1.187.22.
- **DELIVERED ENERGY (v1.187.3).** On an actuated night `delivered_kwh` is the energy into the home Cores over the hold span (§7 "Delivered energy"), and `delivered_basis` records the method: `source-charge` (`DELIVERED_BASIS`), written exactly when `delivered_kwh` is a number. A NULL `delivered_kwh` has a NULL basis and a `Delivered:` clause in `score_notes` naming why. **Rows captured before v1.187.3 carry `delivered_basis` NULL beside a number and are kept as recorded:** their `delivered_kwh` is window grid import minus the concurrent house load, which assumed the house ran on the grid for the whole hold and under-counts every night the SHP2 carried the house from the pack inside it (2026-09-30: 4.71 kWh against ~20.3 kWh into the Cores). The buy de-bias learner reads only the current basis (§15.12 "Announced-buy de-bias").
- **THE CHARGE CYCLE.** The row stays keyed by `plan_date` and its plan columns hold the cost leg (chapter 15 part 2, "The charge cycle record"). `window_kind` (plan) is the cost leg's window, `overnight` on every row until the mid-day switch and NULL on rows written before the cycle record. One list (`CHARGE_CYCLE_LEDGER_SCHEMA`) feeds both the column allowlist and the column-add pass, so no column can be added and then dropped from every write. Columns of a leg or step that did not happen read NULL.
- **THE MID-DAY CYCLE IN SHADOW.** Until the switch, each evening's row also records what the mid-day cycle would have done (chapter 15 part 2, "The mid-day cycle in shadow"), in its own columns, so no live column ever holds a shadow figure: `shadow_cycle_why` (the chooser's reason; `midday` when both legs were sized) and `shadow_computed_at_ms`; the backup leg's `shadow_backup_window_start_ms` / `_end_ms`, `shadow_backup_arms`, `shadow_backup_setpoint_pct`, `shadow_backup_buy_kwh`, `shadow_backup_expected_buy_kwh` and its walk's `shadow_backup_cushion_trough_soc_pct` / `_line_soc_pct`; the cost leg's `shadow_cost_window_start_ms` / `_end_ms`, `shadow_cost_arms`, `shadow_cost_reserve_pct` (T_mid) with `shadow_cost_reserve_basis`, `shadow_cost_level_pct`, `shadow_cost_ceiling_soc_pct` (C_mid), `shadow_cost_buy_kwh`, `shadow_cost_expected_buy_kwh` and `shadow_cost_expected_start_soc_pct`; `shadow_arm_disposition` (why a leg would or would not arm); the morning refresh's `shadow_refreshed_at_ms`, `shadow_refresh_disposition`, `shadow_refreshed_level_pct`, `shadow_refreshed_reserve_pct`, `shadow_refreshed_buy_kwh` and `shadow_refreshed_expected_buy_kwh`, and how old the weather it was sized on was, `shadow_refresh_weather_age_s`. Not every row's four refreshed figures (level, reserve, worst-case and expected buy) are a re-plan's, so they are read with `shadow_refresh_disposition`: a `stood` row carries the evening's figures (no refresh finished in time), a `not-armed` row (since v1.205.1) the evening's figures of a cycle that was not armed (nothing was re-sized, and no charge or refresh followed), and a `not-comparable` row leaves all four NULL; no scorer reads these columns (the de-bias learner reads the live `refreshed_buy_kwh` only). `shadow_window_pv_p50_kwh` / `shadow_window_load_p50_kwh` are the PV and house load the mid-day leg was planned on over its window, and the row's capture measures the same window into `shadow_window_pv_kwh` / `shadow_window_load_kwh` (`shadowWindowActuals`: the home Cores' PV and the panel's load, each at least 90% covered, else NULL; and NULL for a side held an hour or more of the window, "Held telemetry" below) — forecast skill for the hours a mid-day charge would run in, not a validation of the sizing model.   - `arm_disposition` — why an armed night never became an actuation. `actuated` alone is over-loaded: `NULL` covers at least five dispositions (superseded by a later plan, held below the minimum buy, no window resolved, advisory mode, apply guards refused). The 2026-08-29 row sat at `actuated=NULL` with `buy_kwh=36` and read like a failed 36 kWh buy when it was a routine Sat/Sun shared-window supersede — a log line recorded it, the ledger did not.
  - `cost_ceiling_basis` — `'pv-headroom' | 'max-soc' | NULL`. **The field that makes cost mode auditable.** `objective` only records which mode was *configured* plus buy/no-buy; `costModeTargetKwh` floors at the resilience answer and raises only under a strict inequality, so a row labelled `cost_arbitrage` can carry **zero** cost-mode contribution. This value was computed and thrown away, which is why "is cost mode actually doing anything" could not be answered from the ledger.

**Cost columns (v1.187.0).** `realized_cost_cents` is **written**: metered `grid_home_w` import (positive-clamped, gaps over 1 h skipped — the kWh columns' rules) priced interval by interval at `rateAt(tariffModelFromEnv(), t).centsPerKwh` (the plan in effect; `pricedImport`), over the night's scored span — from the window open (or the reserve write, when a pre-v1.187.0 schedule applied it earlier) to window close + 16 h: the overnight buy, the day's off-peak import and the governed on-peak (`realizedCostOutcome`, assembled by `assembleNightLedgerColumns`). Energy only (no basic-service charge). NULL on unconfirmed rates or any interval without a rate, under 90% `grid_home_w` coverage of the span, or a row superseded for its window (the later row carries it); **a `Cost:` clause in `score_notes` always says which** — the priced total, `rates unconfirmed`, `no <season> rate configured for <period>`, `coverage N% < 90%`, or `carried by the <date> plan`. Rates are those configured at capture time; the plan-time rates stay in `tariff_snapshot`.

> **The column is not summable to a bill.** Row spans do not tile time: on a weekday 21:00–23:00 falls in no row (one row ends at close + 16 h = 21:00, the next opens at 23:00), and a Friday row (window Fri 23:00 → Sat 00:00) ends Saturday 16:00, so Saturday 16:00 → Monday 00:00 falls in none. Spans also differ in length (17 h on a Friday row, 21 h on a Monday-window row). A month's sum misses ~2 h every weekday and ~32 h every weekend; the daily grid cost is the HA **Grid Cost Today** sensor.
>
> **A missing season rate nulls whole rows.** `pricedImport` never half-prices a span, and `TARIFF_APS_SUPEROFFPEAK_WINTER_CENTS` defaults to `""`: on a confirmed table without it, every row whose span holds a winter weekday 10:00–15:00 records NULL from October — the first winter usage month, v1.187.10 (the note names the period). The boot logs such gaps once (`unpricedTariffPeriods`: every period, per season it applies in, with no rate on a confirmed table).

Several declared columns are *never written* and read NULL by design: `actual_grid_to_battery_kwh`, `outage_during_day`, `demand_charge_savings_cents` (APS R-EV has no demand charge), and the counterfactual columns `counterfactual_cost_cents`, `realized_savings_cents` and `would_have_peak_imported`. The reasons first given for the counterfactuals (no actuated nights, rates unconfirmed) no longer hold; the one that remains is that each needs the no-buy trajectory, and the recorded one does not supply it: shifting the measured trajectory down by the delivered energy assumes the panel would have discharged the same way from a lower pool, and the SHP2 does not — after it stops at its reserve it resumes discharging only ~20 points above it (2026-09-13/14/27/28), so a lower pool can sit idle and buy grid through hours the measured night discharged. A modelled no-buy day would be an estimate in columns the gate and the owner read as measurements. The grid cost of every day, in dollars, is the HA **Grid Cost Today** sensor (chapter 7 §4, `computeTariffReport`).

**★ Never pruned.** The table is created alongside `lifetime_totals` and is never referenced by the hourly `samples` prune — the multi-month verification record the write-readiness gate reduces over **must survive the samples retention** (design §0.1, binding; 30 days by default, `RECORDER_RETENTION_DAYS` since v1.51.0). The companion `night_charge_calibration` singleton (`id=1 CHECK`; seasonal cushion learner / buy de-bias / EV-tail inflation state, design §3.4) has schema and read/write API but **no live producer or consumer yet** — the learner is a later increment.

**Write path.** `nightUpsert` builds a dynamic `INSERT … ON CONFLICT(plan_date) DO UPDATE` where column names come *only* from a frozen whitelist (`NIGHT_LEDGER_COLUMNS`), never caller keys — that is what makes the interpolation injection-safe. Only columns present and not `undefined` are written, so a partial outcome merge preserves every frozen plan column; prepared statements are cached per column-shape; JS booleans are coerced to INTEGER 0/1 (`node:sqlite` rejects a boolean bind). `recordNightPlanRow` (`index.ts:4042`) coerces nullable plan numerics to 0 for the non-null plan columns (`target_soc_pct`, `buy_kwh`, `required_extra_kwh`) but *omits* `min_proj_soc_pct` when null so the sentinel survives. The synthetic SN `night_charge` is reserved for optional disposable chart-overlay samples (subject to the configured samples retention) and joins the restart-gap exclusion list (like `weather`/`forecast`) so off-cadence wall-clock rows can never mask a real telemetry stall; the durable ledger itself never rides the `samples` table.

### 7. Completion-gated outcome scoring

#### The night clock (`nightWindowBounds`, `nightChargeAdvisor.ts:3659`)

The single source of truth for a plan date's spans, in site-time arithmetic from the plan date's site midnight (pure, null on a malformed date): with the APS R-EV preset, plan for day `D` covers charge window `[D 23:00, D+1 05:00)`; the plan-trajectory score span runs `[D+1 05:00, D+1 21:00)`; the night is **complete** — and may be outcome-captured — only at/after `D+1 21:00` (`completeMs`, = day-start + 45 h); another plan's night is its schedule's fallback night (section 2). `nightSpansForRow` (`index.ts:4184`) prefers the row's **own stored window** when present: the score span then runs **16 h past the real window close** (`window_end_ms + 16 h` — the same close+16 h shape as the canonical 05:00→21:00), which is what pairs a weekend plan's actuals to its Monday-morning window instead of the canonical night. Rows without a stored window (`pre-v1.39.0`) are marked `windowKnown=false` and captured honestly as `scored=0` — actuals cannot be paired to the plan's real window, and a fabricated cross-span comparison must never enter the gate's evidence.

**★ The completion gate** (v1.39.0): a night may be captured only once `nowMs ≥ completeMs`. The pre-fix midnight tick captured yesterday's row while its charge window was still *open* — window coverage ~25% ⇒ `scored=0`, inverted SoC query ⇒ null actuals — and the idempotence latch then froze those truncated actuals into the never-pruned ledger forever, so the readiness gate could never accumulate a single scored night.

**★ …and the forecast span** (v1.187.10): the sweep captures a row only once `nowMs ≥ ledgerCaptureDueMs(completeMs, issued_at_ms)` = `max(completeMs, issued_at_ms + FORECAST_SPAN_MS)` (24 h; `ledgerRowsDueForCapture`, nightLedgerScoring.ts). The PV/load actuals are integrated over `[issued_at_ms, +24 h]` clipped to the capture instant (`forecastActualsSpan`), so a capture before issue + 24 h froze a short actual against a 24 h forecast. On a weekday the gap was 0.1–0.5 h (completion ~21:00, issue ~21:30); a Friday 1 h window closes Saturday 00:00 and completes Saturday 16:00, ~5.4 h short. The 2026-10-02 row was captured at 16:08:08 on 18.6 h of load: 39.62 kWh against an 81.8 kWh forecast, `load_err_frac` −0.52, `buy_err_kwh` +51.09 kWh — a positive skew of ~10–12 kWh on every such Friday row, the direction that masks a real under-buy from the HARD criterion, and an inflated \|`load_err_frac`\| in the load-band calibration. `completeMs` itself is unchanged (the premature-capture repair below resets any row captured before it, across a 60-day sweep that can reach past the samples retention). Consequences: a weekday row now captures at the first sweep after issue + 24 h (~21:30–22:00), usually the 30-min tick after that evening's job, so the evening plan's ledger-derived calibrations and the evening notification's readiness see the previous night one sweep later; the tick that captures it also recomputes the readiness the apply moment reads (the window opens at 23:00 or later). A Friday row captures Saturday ~21:30–22:00. Known limit: the P50 sums the forecast's 24 hourly slots from the next whole hour after issue, so the actual span leads the forecast span by up to 1 h; the lengths are equal.

#### A two-leg row (`cycleLedgerSpans`)

A row whose cost leg is a mid-day window (`window_kind` `super_off_peak`, none before the switch) scores three spans, whether or not its backup armed: the backup span from its overnight open to the mid-day open, the window, and the post span from the close to the next chargeable open (`next_recharge_ms`), at most 16 hours — 15:00 to 23:00 on a weekday. The rows then tile end to end, so the 05:00–10:00 low falls inside one; a plain close + 16 hours would end at 07:00, inside the next row's backup span. The realized trough (`actual_min_soc_pct`, `cushion_breached`) is the lowest over the backup and post spans (`minOverSpans`, the median-of-3 filter per span), so a 07:00 breach on an actuated cycle is a strike, armed backup or not — the gate keeps its rule. The on-peak attribution counts both cheap periods for such a row (`ledgerCheapPeriodIds`), and a later row whose backup window is this row's window supersedes it (`supersedingPlanDate`: Sunday's mid-day cycle holds Monday 00:00–05:00 as its backup). The row completes once its post span and its governed on-peak have closed: a weekday row captures at 23:00 the next day, so readiness lags one evening. The readiness gate keeps every criterion pooled and publishes the graduation count per window kind (`actuatedNightsOvernight`, `actuatedNightsMidday`).

#### The backfill sweep (`scoreCompletedNights`, `index.ts:4233`)

Runs on the 30-min tick, the evening job, and the boot warm-up. Sweeps the last **60 days** of ledger rows (not exactly-yesterday — the old scorer orphaned any night left uncaptured across SHP2-wedge/downtime days forever, an uncounted MNAR exclusion), skipping rows already captured (`outcome_captured_at_ms != null` — idempotent) and rows still in flight. A night whose raw telemetry has aged past the configured samples retention (default 30 days) captures honestly as `scored=0` with null actuals — it does not stay uncaptured forever.

#### Measuring one night (`scoreNightRow`, `index.ts:4280`)

- **Grid import**: trapezoidal integration of SHP2 `grid_home_w` (positive-clamped, gaps > 1 h skipped; `integrateWh` / `coverageFrac`, nightLedgerScoring.ts) over the real charge window and — v1.187.0 — over the on-peak period the plan governs (`nightSpansForRow` → `ledgerSpansForWindow` → `governedOnPeakSpan`; §6 "ON-PEAK OUTCOME"; `nightOnPeakOutcome` writes the value, `onpeak_start_ms`/`_end_ms` and `onpeak_basis`, and appends an `On-peak:` clause to `score_notes`). It was the plan day's own 16:00–19:00 until v1.187.0. A row superseded for its window (`supersedingPlanDate`: a later `plan_date` with the identical stored window) records no on-peak and no cost, and queries neither span. The completion boundary is `max(window close + 16 h, governed on-peak end)` (`ledgerSpansForWindow`); under R-EV the governed on-peak (16:00–19:00 after a 05:00 close) always ends inside the 16 h span, so no capture moves. **Null-over-fabrication**: zero samples record NULL, never a "measured" 0 kWh.
- **The assembly is pure** (v1.187.0): the on-peak, realized-cost, delivered-energy and PV set-aside columns come from `assembleNightLedgerColumns`, which reads the house panel's series only through a `query(metric, start, end)` seam; `scoreNightRow` runs it against the recorder and writes the result (`ledgerScorerAssembly.test.ts` drives it with a stub).
- **Realized cost** (v1.187.0): `realized_cost_cents` — §6 "Cost columns". **Delivered energy** on an actuated night integrates the span the write was actually held (`deliveredHoldSpan`), from the write (or the window open, whichever is earlier) to: the restore's own time, `actuation_reverted_at_ms` (stamped by the actuator from v1.187.0), clamped to `[close, close + HOLD_TAIL_MAX_MS]` (30 min — past that the span would integrate the solar morning, where import − load turns negative), plus `HOLD_DEVICE_SETTLE_MS` (1 min); with no stamp and a write before the window (the pre-v1.187.0 schedule), close + `LEGACY_REVERT_LAG_MS` (5 min) as before; with no stamp on the current schedule, close + `ACTUATOR_TICK_MS` + `HOLD_DEVICE_SETTLE_MS`. A span ending exactly at the close dropped the restore's own tick (up to ~60 s) plus the device's stop — 0.1–0.5 kWh at 16–19 kW on a night still charging at the close, always toward under-delivery, in the column the buy de-bias calibrator trains on (the v1.115.0 defect class). A cancel or grid-loss abort keeps the window, as before.
- **What delivered energy measures** (v1.187.3, `deliveredIntoCores`): the energy into the home Cores — the charging part (> 0) of the house panel's source channels (`src{n}_w`, from `backupInfo.chWatt`; positive = the panel charging that Core, negative = the Core carrying the house), integrated over the hold span and summed. **The channels come from the hold:** every source channel (`SOURCE_CHANNEL_SLOTS`, slots 1–3) with at least one recorded sample inside the hold, plus every slot the house panel reports connected at capture (`houseConnectedSlots` → `connectedSourceSlots`: connected and carrying a serial — the live rule of `panelRoster`, without its persisted `lastRoster` fallback). Capture runs ~16 h after the close, so membership at that moment is not the night's: a Core unplugged or in service by then, or a `/quota/all` that came back without the `pd303_mc` sources subtree that minute, would otherwise drop a channel that charged all night (or every channel) from a column written once. The recorder writes `src{n}_w` for every `chWatt` entry whether or not the slot is connected, so an empty slot reads zeros and adds nothing, and the slot ↔ `chWatt` index mapping does not affect the total. A slot connected at capture whose channel recorded nothing over the hold is still read, as a dark channel, so the coverage gate below withholds the night. Until v1.187.3 the column was window grid import minus the concurrent house load (`actuatedDeliveredKwh`, removed), which assumed the house ran on the grid for the whole hold; when the SHP2 carries the house from the pack — above its reserve before a just-in-time force-charge, and after the force-charge's OFF — the subtraction removed load the grid never carried. 2026-09-30: import 23.38 − load 18.67 = 4.71 kWh, against 20.34 kWh on the source channels (the Cores' own AC input 20.25, the pool 55→76%). On nights the house rode the grid all window the two agree to within ~3% on 09-24 (23.64 vs 24.26 kWh) and 09-28 (46.26 vs 47.41) and ~10% on the small 09-29 night (7.23 vs 7.93). A channel carrying the house reads negative and adds nothing, so hours on the pack cost nothing and hours on the grid count only what reached the Cores. NULL, with a `Delivered:` clause in `score_notes`, when no channel recorded anything over the hold and no connected Core is known; when any channel covers less than `LEDGER_SPAN_MIN_COVERAGE` (0.9) of the hold — the minimum over channels, as `actual_pv_kwh`'s per-core gate, since one dark channel in three averages to a healthy 0.67 while the total is a third short; or when the total exceeds the metered `grid_home_w` import over the hold (when that is itself ≥ 0.9 covered) by more than `DELIVERED_IMPORT_SLACK_FRAC` (5%) + `DELIVERED_IMPORT_SLACK_KWH` (0.5 kWh) — overnight the grid is the only source that can charge the Cores, so more than that is a channel read with the wrong sign or meaning. `HOLD_TAIL_MAX_MS` is unchanged; the morning bias it was sized against was import − load's.
- **Coverage**: fraction of 5-min buckets in the window with ≥ 1 sample; `< 0.9` ⇒ `scored=0` ("MNAR-excluded").
- **PV/load actuals** are integrated over `[issued_at_ms, +24 h]` — the same horizon the frozen P50 forecast covered, so `pv_err_frac`/`load_err_frac` are like-for-like. Actual PV = Σ `pv_total` over the SHP2-connected home DPUs (the same basis the forecast is built from); actual load = SHP2 `panel_load`. v1.187.10: the span is whole at capture (the forecast-span gate above); before it a row captured earlier integrated only to the capture instant.
- **A panel row's PV actual** (v1.204.0, `panelRowPvActual`, `panelPvAdmission`): per slot of its `pv_basis_slots`, the panel's own reading (`src{n}_pv_w`) for a P slot and the slot's Core's `pv_total` for a C slot, over the same `[issued_at_ms, +24 h]`, each slot at the Cores' coverage minimum, withheld on held telemetry. A P slot's day reaches the column only if the 7-day energy balance admitted it, as of an evening after the span (the one predicate the planning fit, bias and skill read). The span's last day is the day after the plan's evening, which no balance can judge before the next evening's report, built after that day's daily total from the panel's own ledger comes in (06:35–09:00): until the main thread has read such a report (`panelAdmissionMem`, the newest read, never an earlier evening's), the row is captured at the usual time with its PV actual **pending** — the cushion verdict and the strikes as usual, the PV-dependent columns empty, the note marked "PV actual pending the energy balance" — and `repairPendingPanelPv` resets it for re-capture once the report is in (inside the re-capture horizon). Then the actual is written, or **withheld** with the slot-day the balance did not pass. The note names the sources and the evening the balance passed the span's days as of, with the plan's own stored ratio as of its evening: "PV actual is the panel's own per-slot reading for slots 1, 2 and 3, on days the 7-day energy balance passed as of `<evening>` (the newest evening it had judged as of when the row was captured). The plan's own 7-day energy balance read `<R>` as of `<plan date>`, the evening it was made. The row is kept apart from the readiness gate's sizing figures." A pending row whose next report never comes (the mode set to advisory, which asks for none) keeps its note and an empty PV actual.
- **Minimum SoC** over the score span passes through `medianFilter3` (3-sample median; endpoints pass through) *before* the min-scan: the documented SHP2 cloud-reconnect artifact emits a single transient `backupBatPercent=0` sample, and an unfiltered min-scan latches it — fabricating a realized need equal to the full floor+cushion and a false HARD under-buy in the gate's evidence. A genuine sustained low has agreeing neighbors and passes.
- **Realized-need buy** — the hindsight counterfactual `buy_err_kwh` is scored against — is defensible **only on a clean night**: in advisory phase the home runs *without* the buy, and on a grid-tied home its own overnight import props the SoC. `scored=1` requires: plan had a trajectory (`min_proj_soc_pct` non-null) ∧ coverage ≥ 0.9 ∧ a min-SoC read exists ∧ window import ≤ `ARB_MIN_BUY_KWH` (near-zero — "clean islanded baseline"). Then `realizedNeedBuyKwh = max(0, targetFloorKwh − actualMinPackKwh)/legEff`. Every other case records `scored=0` with an explicit `score_notes` reason.
- **The floor verdict is the plan trajectory, never baseline telemetry** (design §3.3): `plan_traj_floor_breached` = the plan's own sized trough below its own line — v1.186.0: `cushionTroughSocPct < cushionLineSocPct` (the trough that set `cushionShortfall`, and reserve floor + the cushion applied; `legacy-pct` keeps the whole-house `minProjSocPct` against floor + `cushionPct`; no recorded trough ⇒ null). Before v1.186.0 it compared the whole-house disclosure trough, which reads 0% on every cost-mode night, so the gate counted every such night as a strike; a pre-v1.186 row with `cushion_shortfall` 0 and no trough is set aside by `nightChargeGate`. In advisory phase raw min-SoC telemetry measures the un-actuated baseline, not the plan's line. Raw telemetry feeds only `soc_min_err_pct` (plan minus actual) and the separate `cushion_breached` observation. v1.196.0 — `cushion_breached` is graded on the claim the plan made (`realizedCushionBreach`). Where the cushion is an outage starting at the close (an overnight row whose `cushion_line_soc_pct` is not `reserve_floor_pct + cushion_pct`), it is the pool at the close (`window_close_soc_pct`) under the line, less 0.05 points. Without that reading it is 0 when the lowest pool after the close holds the line, and NULL otherwise. Elsewhere it is, as before, the lowest pool over the scored span under `reserve_floor_pct + cushion_pct`. `score_notes` states the reading against the line. Chapter 15 part 2 has the gate's rule. `pv_in_band`/`load_in_band` check the actual against the frozen `[P10, P90]`; `onpeak_import_occurred` uses a 0.05 kWh threshold.

#### Premature-capture repair (`repairPrematureNightOutcomes`, `index.ts:5220`)

Once per boot, idempotent: any row whose `outcome_captured_at_ms` **precedes its own night's `completeMs`** (the pre-v1.39.0 mid-window defect) has its outcome/score columns reset to NULL (`scored=0`, explanatory `score_notes`) so the now-completion-gated sweep re-captures it with full-span actuals. Rows are corrected in place — never deleted.

#### Forecast-span repair (`repairShortForecastSpanOutcomes`, v1.187.10)

Once per boot, idempotent, before the warm sweep: `forecastSpanRepair` (nightLedgerScoring.ts) selects captured rows with a stored window whose capture preceded `issued_at_ms + 24 h` by at least `FORECAST_SPAN_REPAIR_MIN_SHORT_MS` (1 h) — Friday 1 h windows (~5.4 h short) and evening jobs delayed past ~22:00.
- **Inside the re-capture horizon** — `issued_at_ms` within `min(RECORDER_RETENTION_DAYS, 60) − 2` days (`forecastSpanRecaptureHorizonMs`; 28 days at the default 30-day retention, 58 days with multi-year retention, so the row keeps its raw samples and stays inside the 60-day sweep) — the outcome columns are reset as the premature-capture repair resets them (`forecastSpanResetColumns`: capture stamp NULL, `scored=0`, a `reset —` note) and the sweep re-captures the row over the full span. A re-capture re-scores every outcome column on the current scorer — the delivered-energy basis, the governed on-peak, the PV coverage gate and the Cores connected at capture — like any late capture. One info line per row names the shortfall and the captured load and `buy_err_kwh`.
- **Not re-scorable on today's devices** (review, `forecastSpanRecapturable`) — a row inside the horizon is tagged like one past it when it has no `pv_model_sns` (planned before v1.187.0, or with no recorded model set: `pvVerdictSetAside` cannot see a roster change, and a re-capture sums actual PV over today's panel-connected Cores — before the 2026-08-20 roster change another Core held slot 3), or when it carries a `delivered_kwh` with no `delivered_basis` (captured before v1.187.3: the buy de-bias learner reads those as legacy and they are never rewritten). The live 2026-10-02 row carries both and is re-captured.
- **Past it** the row is kept as captured and `score_notes` gains a `Forecast span:` clause stating the hours covered and that the PV/load errors under-count (`forecastSpanTagNote`; the marker makes it idempotent). One summary line lists the tagged rows. The tag is not read by the readiness gate or the load-band calibration; such rows age out of the 120-day band window.
- **Not repaired:** weekday rows captured before v1.187.10, 0.1–0.5 h short, are kept as captured (a re-capture is spent only where the PV/load/buy columns are materially wrong).

#### Held telemetry (`heldLedgerVerdict`, v1.188.1)

A device whose readings were held — offline in the EcoFlow cloud with its last reading kept in the store, or a panel whose body the cloud replays — reports nothing fresh. Before v1.188.1 the recorder restated such a reading every 5 minutes, and the per-Core PV coverage gate counted the restated rows as coverage. After the 2026-10-14 internet outage every Core was held for about 20 h. On that timeline the v1.188.0 scorer, capturing the 10-14 row after 10-15 21:30, finds each Core's span about 0.92 covered (only the two outage hours missing), which passes the gate, and integrates the held 0 W of PV over a day the pool charged to about 96 % on solar: `actual_pv_kwh` about 0, `pv_err_frac` about −1 and `pv_in_band` 0, which the readiness gate reads whether or not the row is scored. The row is left unscored only because about two of its six window hours have no grid reading (coverage about 66 %). The 10-15 row, its window covered by the fresh panel and its Cores still held, is scored on that PV.

The recorder no longer writes held readings and moves the ones it had written out of `samples` (architecture chapter, sections 4.3 and 4.4); each hold of an hour or more is in `held_spans`. The scorer reads the spans over the row's PV/load span (`[issued_at_ms, +24 h]`): a home Core held for `HELD_LEDGER_MIN_OVERLAP_MS` (1 h) or more of it leaves `actual_pv_kwh` NULL, the house panel held that long leaves `actual_load_kwh` NULL, and either one makes the row `scored=0` with a `not scored — telemetry held:` note naming the device and the hours. An hour is the trapezoid's own bridge: a shorter hold is integrated across, between two real readings; a longer one would be dropped and the total would under-count. The reason is stated before the coverage reasons. A row left unscored because its plan basis was incomplete keeps the basis reason first and, when its span holds such a hold, states the held verdict after it with the same marker (`basisIncompleteNote`): such a row keeps its window and model Cores, so its actuals are captured and the repair below reads it, and without the marker every sweep reset and re-captured it for the whole horizon. Until the first scan for held rows has finished after a boot, the sweep captures nothing (the 30-min tick retries).

**A cushion-only row (v1.203.0)** is never scored, in any outcome, the Cores' actuals covered included: its buy was sized on the outage cushion with no solar credited, so the planner-sizing metric (the buy against the solar and load forecast) has no solar forecast to grade, and a `buy_err_kwh` of plan + (0 − actual PV) ÷ 0.927 would read as a large over-buy. The branch (`cushionOnlyNote`) comes right after the incomplete-basis one and before the held, coverage and actuals branches: "not scored — a cushion-only plan (no solar basis: …): its buy was sized on the outage cushion at the window close with no solar credited, so the planner-sizing metric has no solar forecast to grade.", with the held verdict and its marker after it where there is one. `realized_need_buy_kwh` and `buy_err_kwh` stay NULL. It teaches nothing of the solar model (the band calibration reads recorded PV, never the ledger), the buy de-bias (it needs `scored = 1`), the under-buy pool or graduation (both `actuated && scored`). It still teaches the load: `load_err_frac` and `load_in_band` are recorded against the published load forecast, which is real. Its realized verdict stands: the pool at the close against its line (`window_close_soc_pct`, `cushion_breached`), so an actuated cushion-only night (or one that stood, or never held its write) that closes under its line less 0.05 is a realized strike, and a line night armed and never switched on is struck by `armedNeverRanUnder`. **The asymmetry:** such a night can add a strike and cannot clear one, since the 14-night streak counts only `actuated && scored` nights — fail-closed; the gate was already BLOCKED and auto already runs as supervised while the Cores are dark, so no write changes. With the panel's point (v1.203.0, part 1) the pool the panel holds under the write is the line's whole percent, so a reserve-held night closes at or above its line.

The mid-day shadow's measured window (`shadow_window_pv_kwh` / `_load_kwh`, v1.189.0) takes the same verdict over the window itself (`shadowWindowActuals`, given `recorder.heldSpans` and the house panel): a home Core held for an hour or more of the 10:00-15:00 window leaves the PV figure NULL, the panel held that long the load figure. The window's 90% coverage already refuses such an hour, since the held readings are not in `samples` and an hour is a fifth of the window; the verdict states the refusal on the scorer's own rule, whatever the window's length. The reset below clears both figures with the outcome columns (`forecastSpanResetColumns`), and the re-capture measures the window again; the shadow's other columns, what the evening computed, are not outcome columns and are kept. A tagged row keeps them as captured, with the row. A row whose PV verdict is set aside for a Core-set mismatch (`pv_verdict_set_aside`: `pvVerdictSetAside` or the known-row list) leaves the PV figure NULL too (v1.189.0 review; the scorer passes `cols.pvSetAside`): the shadow's window P50 comes from the same evening band, so its measured PV is no forecast-skill evidence either. The load figure is kept.

**Repair.** The sweep first resets any captured row whose PV/load span, as captured (`[issued_at_ms, min(capture, issue + 24 h)]`), overlaps such a hold and whose notes do not already state that side as `telemetry held` (`heldLedgerRepair`; per side, below), with the same Cores and panel it then captures with. Inside the forecast-span repair's horizon, and re-capturable on today's devices (`forecastSpanRecapturable`), the outcome columns are reset (`heldResetColumns`: the forecast-span reset's columns, its own note) and the row is re-captured; otherwise `score_notes` gains a `Telemetry held:` clause and the row is kept as captured. Both write the marker, so the repair is idempotent. Rows are corrected in place, never deleted. The reset note and its log line name only the actuals that were integrated over the hold (`heldIntegratedActuals`: PV when a home Core was held, `actual_pv_kwh` was captured non-null and a held `pv_total` row of a held Core lies inside the span as captured; load likewise, with the panel's `panel_load`); the held rows are looked up in `held_samples` (`recorder.heldRowsWithin`). A frozen span runs from the last change before a silence of more than an hour, so a row whose span overlaps only that silence is held by the verdict and integrated no held reading: its note says the actual was integrated over hours with no readings at all and that no held reading lay inside its span. A held side captured empty is said to have been captured empty, so nothing had been integrated. The tag says the span had no fresh readings, "held or missing", and does not claim the actuals were integrated from held readings: a hold can cover hours in which the device wrote no rows at all, and past the horizon the raw telemetry that would tell the two apart is gone. **Per side (v1.188.1, fifth review).** The marker is read per side (`heldSidesNoted`: the `PV — …` and `load — …` parts of every held clause in the notes) and compared with the sides the spans hold now (`heldSides`, the same test the scorer withholds each actual by). A side held but not stated is repaired as above (the hourly scan can find a replayed Core after the capture, which stated only the panel; with one marker for the row, its PV actual stayed integrated from the replay), the tag naming only that side. A side stated but no longer held, with a restored span of one of its devices overlapping the row's span (architecture chapter, section 4.4; `recorder.restoredHeldSpans`; the incident's hold began the day before the 10-15 row's issue and ran past its end), is re-captured from the readings moved back, with a note saying so; past the horizon it is kept as captured. A side still held by another of its devices is not restored: with one of two held Cores restored, the row stays refused and is not reset (a rule reading the restore alone would reset and re-capture it on every sweep, each re-capture stating PV held again). A Core's side is never restored by the panel's restored span. A side no longer held with no restore behind it (a Core no longer on the panel is not held by today's roster either) is left as it is. A side changes at most twice (a span only grows, and a restored span is never held again), so a row is reset at most a few times. The write is one call for both actions (`heldRepairColumns`); the sweep reads the spans over its rows' days even when none is left, and after a reset it reads the ledger again before it captures, so the reset row is re-captured by the same sweep.
