## 1.207.0

### The energy bridge keeps each Core's own capacity, however long the Cores stay dark

**Energy bridge**

- **Why.** While a home Core sends no telemetry, the energy bridge meters its battery and solar at the panel's source slot, and in daylight it counts the slot's stored energy as the panel's percent × the Core's capacity. That capacity came from the recorded `pack<k>_full_cap_mah` rows, read at most 45 days back (and only while the rows survive the samples retention, 30 days by default) and cached for 6 hours. Past the 45 days the bridge counted the slot on its 30,720 Wh nameplate, with no log line. On the reference plant's three slots that is 6.3 %, 8.8 % and 2.8 % more per percent (28,889 / 28,227 / 29,879 Wh held), due at the first daylight window 45 days after the Cores' last pack rows (2026-11-29 for Cores dark since mid-October, on a retention of 45 days or more). The inferred lower bound grows by the same share of its stored-energy term, so an honest panel PV field can be found short of it. A field found short loses its trust: the slot is booked on the inferred basis, the power sensors withhold it for the rest of that day, and the night plan refuses the panel's readings that evening.
- **The capacity source.** A dark slot is counted on its Core's capacity ratio × the slot's nameplate, capped at the nameplate. The ratio comes from a capacity tracker the recorder keeps for the bridge: the Core's own last measured ratio at any age (`held`), else, when the tracker holds none for that Core, the lowest ratio it holds for any Core (`min-known`), else the nameplate, only when it holds no ratio at all (`nameplate`). The 45-day read and the 6-hour cache are removed. The pool figures replace a held ratio older than 30 days with the lowest known one; the bridge does not, so each slot keeps its own Core's figure.
- **A dark Core is never measured.** At each 5-minute rollup the tracker measures every panel's connected Cores that are listed online with a complete pack list and are not dark to the bridge (a Core that delivers a reading of its own; a marked Core once it has handed back), whether or not any slot is dark, so an outage starts from the ratio measured at the last such rollup. A Core listed online on a replayed body, or on its first body after a restart, lists pack figures and is not a reading: it is not measured, and its slot's figure does not move while the slot is dark. A returning Core's ratio is recorded at the first rollup it has handed back at. The tracker's `measured` basis (a ratio read from the pack figures now) is therefore not the basis of a dark slot.
- **No answer is never the nameplate.** When the tracker does not answer, the slot keeps the figure last resolved in that process. With none, or when the panel reports no capacity for the slot, the slot has no capacity figure: the inferred basis and the field check do not run for it, and a trusted field is still booked as measured. A tracker failure at a rollup is contained: the rollup completes and every counter advances.
- **The tracker.** It is the recorder's own (main thread), with its own file, `pool-energy-basis-bridge.json`, beside the other trackers' files. While its own file holds no Core it is seeded, read-only, from the runway tracker's file, and the seeded ratios are written to its own file at that first load, at the instants the seed recorded. Later boots read its own file and do not need the runway tracker's. A failed write of that file is not logged, and is not tried again in that process until a Core is measured (the next boot tries again), so the file's presence after the first rollup is the check that it landed.
- **The log lines.** Each slot's capacity is logged at the first rollup that books the slot (day or night; a rollup that holds its window for a missing panel row logs nothing, and the line follows at the next) and on every change of basis or whole-Wh figure, for example `energy bridge: slot 2 is counted on 28.23 kWh — ×0.9188 of its 30.72 kWh nameplate, its Core's last measured ratio, of <ISO time> (held)`. The instant is the tracker's: the last rollup the Core delivered a reading at, and after a restart the instant last written to the tracker's file, which is re-stamped hourly while the ratio is unchanged (up to an hour before the last reading; the ratio is the same). The other lines end `the lowest ratio the capacity tracker holds: it holds none for this slot's Core (min-known)`, `is counted on its 30.72 kWh nameplate — the capacity tracker holds no ratio for any Core (nameplate)`, `stays counted on … kWh — the capacity tracker did not answer (unavailable)` and `has no capacity figure — …`. The first two say what the tracker holds, not what was ever measured: a ratio in another tracker's file, or one lost with a disabled store, is not held. With more than one panel the line names the panel by name; it never carries a serial. A slot that goes dark a second time logs its line again. Nothing is resolved or logged while no slot is bridged. A line that cannot be written does not stop the rollup.
- **The debug block.** The bridge block of `/api/debug/battery-lifetime` gains `capacity`: per bridged dark slot, `wh`, `ratio`, `basis` and `atMs` (the tracker's instant, as in the line). `pvChecks[].stepWh` is `wh` / 100.
- **At deploy.** On a supervised install whose Cores' ratios are in the runway tracker's file and whose last pack rows were still being read (inside the 45 days and the samples retention), the figures do not move, and each slot's line ends `(held)`: ratio × nameplate is the pack rows' sum where the packs' design capacity sums to the slot's nameplate (five packs of 6,144 Wh on the reference plant; 28,889 / 28,227 / 29,879 Wh by the ratios in that file). On the reference plant it was read back before the release: on 2026-11-15 the running v1.205.1 build's step × 100 (28,888.99 / 28,226.97 / 29,878.58 Wh) equalled ratio × nameplate to under 0.01 Wh on each slot. The figures move in five cases, each named by its log line.
  - Pack rows no longer read at the update (older than 45 days, or pruned by the samples retention): the figure returns from the nameplate to the Core's own.
  - No ratio of the Core's own in the tracker: a secondary panel's Cores (their ratios are in the secondary panels' tracker file, which is not the seed) and a Core no tracker ever measured take the lowest ratio the tracker holds.
  - Persistence disabled (`POOL_ENERGY_BASIS_DIR` empty, or unsupervised with it unset): the nameplate after a restart while the Cores are dark, where the pack rows had covered 45 days.
  - No readable tracker file on a supervised install (neither the bridge's own nor the runway tracker's): the nameplate, where pack rows inside the 45 days had been used.
  - A slot whose panel reports no capacity: no capacity figure, where the pack rows' sum had been used. While its field is not trusted nothing is booked for it in daylight (no solar, no battery energy), and the field is not checked.
- **Known limits at a Core's return.** Each can cost a slot's field its trust on the day it happens; none arises while every Core stays dark.
  - A capacity figure that changes while its slot stays bridged restates that day's percent change on the new figure in the window where it changes. A Core's own ratio does not change while its slot is dark. The figure of a slot counted on the lowest ratio the tracker holds, or on the nameplate, follows another Core's reading at once: on the reference ratios one Core's return raises it by up to 2.4 %, which revokes a trusted field reading under about 0.87 of its bound.
  - A reading the tracker does not measure. The recorded pack rows hold every reading; the tracker measures at a 5-minute rollup, and only a Core listed online. A Core that reports between two rollups, or over MQTT while it is listed offline, and is dark again by the next rollup leaves its slot on the older ratio, where v1.205.1 read the newer rows. Where the newer reading is lower by about 2.4 % or more, a field reading 0.87 of its bound is revoked, and stays revoked while that ratio stands.
  - A Core that hands back at a rollup that holds its window, and is dark again by the next, keeps the day's earlier starting point with its new ratio, so a higher ratio restates the day in one window. v1.205.1 does the same at its next cache refresh.
- **Not changed.**
  - No panel write and no write's timing. No planner code or input is edited (`index.ts` is not edited): the only route from the bridge to a plan is the field's trust, which follows the capacity figure as before.
  - The 7-day balance and the just-in-time force-charge start, which keep the panel's nameplate.
  - The field check's rule and constants (8 steps of evidence, max(2 steps, 15 %), the one-hour hold, the 2 kWh margin) and every existing bridge log line.
  - The other three capacity trackers: their figures, files, seeding and persistence.
  - The `pack<k>_full_cap_mah` rows are still recorded.
  - A revoked field still leaves its log line only: no record, no notice.

## 1.206.0

### A charge window that opens a day or more out is no longer announced as tonight

**Night charge**

- **Why.** On the APS tariff a Saturday has no overnight window of its own: the 21:30 job on a Saturday plans Monday's 12 AM–5 AM window, 26.5 hours out, and the Sunday before a Monday holiday plans Tuesday's. The notice worded every shape from the evening it was sent, never from the window. On 2026-11-14 its title read "Night-charge: charging to ~90% tonight (~46.6 kWh)" and its body "Charging the backup pool to ~90% overnight", while its own cancel deadline ("on Monday at 12:00 AM"), the spoken notice and the window sensors named Monday, and no write was due before Monday. The title has carried "tonight" since the advisory notices were introduced, and was last reworded in 1.187.22. On the fixture year 52 of 365 evenings are such evenings (46 Saturdays, 6 Sundays before a Monday holiday), each 26.5 hours before its window.
- **The rule.** A window is worded this way when it opens 24 hours or more after the plan was made (`siteTime.windowBeyondTonight`): the instant from which the cancel deadline and the window sensors already name the weekday. Inside 24 hours every word of every notice is as it was.
- **The notice names the window.** "charging to ~90% Monday 12am–5am (~46.6 kWh)"; in advisory mode "charge to ~90% recommended for Monday 12am–5am (~46.6 kWh)"; a reserve hold "reserve held at 37% Monday 12am–5am — no charge expected". A charge or a reserve hold opens "No charge is planned for tonight: the charge window opens Monday at 12:00 AM." and says "in that window" where it said "overnight".
- **A hold, a refused arm and a missing plan say what is armed.** For such a window they said "nothing will be charged", "The add-on will not charge tonight" and "The add-on will not raise the reserve tonight". A later evening's job can plan the same window again, so each was a statement about a decision not yet made. They now say what is true when the notice is sent: "so nothing is armed for it.", "Nothing is armed for it." and "Nothing is armed for that window." Titles: "hold for Monday 12am–5am (no charge needed)", "hold for Monday 12am–5am (the need is under the minimum buy)", "no charge Monday 12am–5am (the window cannot cover the cushion)", "planned charge for Monday 12am–5am NOT armed", "planned reserve hold for Monday 12am–5am NOT armed", "no plan for Monday 12am–5am". A plan with no window, and a night with no pool readings, keep their words: there is no window to name.
- **An armed notice says whose figures it carries.** It ends "The figures above are this evening's; a later evening run that arms the same window replaces this arm." An advisory, refused, hold or no-plan notice does not carry the sentence, and nothing is said of a later run that does not arm.
- **The island lows.** "the low SoC after that window" where it said "tomorrow's low SoC"; one figure where the two are the same ("~0% with or without this charge", where it read "~0% without this charge, ~0% with it"); "this write" for "tonight's write" on a reserve hold. No day is named beside the figures.
- **Notes and the supervised sentence.** "that window's 52.2% outage line" for "tonight's"; "Resilience needs no buy in that window"; "the PV headroom of the morning after that window"; "if the car charges in that window". Which notes appear, and in what order, is unchanged. A refusal reason, a cushion-only basis sentence and a panel-basis sentence pass through as they are.
- **The card.** A charge plan whose window opens a day or more after the card is drawn shows it with its weekday ("in the Mon 00:00–Mon 05:00 window"), and a line under the figure reads "No charge is planned for tonight — the charge window opens Mon 00:00." The banner of a write armed for such a window gives its deadline with the weekday ("reserve → 50% at Mon 00:00"), its button reads "Cancel this write", and after a cancel it reads "Cancelled — the armed write is withdrawn." A cancel made before the last evening run ahead of a window (for Monday 12 AM, any time before Sunday's 21:30 run, whatever the button then reads) withdraws that evening's arm only: that run plans the window again and announces what it arms.
- **Unchanged.** Text only. Nothing written to the panel, no write time, no force-charge start or stop and no kWh figure changes: of `server/src` only `notify.ts` (lines added, none changed or removed), `chargeText.ts` and `siteTime.ts` differ, and no plan-identity or golden fixture moves. Every 21:30 notice for a window inside 24 hours, and every spoken notice at any distance, is byte-identical. Measured on the fixture year on 1.205.1's source (commit 8f4b6748), before any source line changed, and again on this release's source: the same counts and hashes on both, and the suite asserts them on every run:
  - 27,028 plans (23,388 inside 24 hours, 3,640 beyond): 104,802 notices inside 24 hours, sha256 `9b69b1a1…9f78`; 41,350 spoken notices, sha256 `ce9a96d3…1c5f`.
  - The armed and advisory 21:30 notices of the plans at 4–20 hours on both pools that are inside 24 hours, with every armed mid-day render of that sweep: 58,514, sha256 `e4789235…1e3e`.
  - The mid-day renders of that sweep with the line gate on and off, with and without the backup leg, and the gate-off night's 21:30 notices inside 24 hours: 452,098, sha256 `eedf2d22…4121`.
- **Not in this release.** For such a window the plan rationale keeps "tonight's write" and "tonight's buy"; the card keeps its hold, no-plan and refused leads and its outage-line and EV rows; a card that cannot read its status keeps "Cancel tonight" and "Cancelled — no write tonight." at any distance; the morning summary and the log lines keep their words. From 24 hours before its window a write cancelled earlier reads "Cancelled — no write tonight.", as every cancelled write does inside 24 hours, although an evening run still to come can arm that window again. A later evening that decides to hold clears an earlier arm for the same window that has not been applied and has nothing still open on the panel, and with `NIGHT_CHARGE_NOTIFY_ON_HOLD` set to `false` it does so with a log line and no notice.
