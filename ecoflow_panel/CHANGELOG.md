## 1.200.0

### An outage-cushion line above the panel's 50% reserve is force-charged to the window close, and disclosed where it cannot be held

**Night charge**

- The panel accepts a backup reserve of at most 50%. On a charge night whose islanded-outage line, asked to the whole percent above it, is above 50, the reserve was written at 50 and the plan still claimed the cushion: `cushionShortfall` did not count the write envelope. Over a year of live-shaped nights 13 charge plans on the nameplate line and 24 on the measured one at a scale of 0.944 claimed the cushion this way. On 10 and 15 of them no cost force-charge stop sat at or above the line, so the pool closed the window near 50%, under the line, and the realized cushion verdict recorded an engine-fault strike. One of those said nothing at all about the 50% write: on 2026-10-29 21:30 at 0.944 (a 30% floor and 8 h of a 2.94 kW island) the line was 65.95%, and the setpoint and arrival were both 66%. On 2027-01-04 21:30 (line 55.55%) the headline read "Hold the reserve at 50% … no charge expected".
- **The fix.** Such a plan, when it is a charge night and not economic-only:
  - is **armed** with the night force-charge to the window close in a write mode (`supervised` or `auto`), on any leg but the outage backup leg, whatever the ask. The plan carries `forceChargeLine` (the ask and the line);
  - is **carried** (the line claimed) only with an ask of 90 or less, and an expected close at or above the line less 0.05 unless its sizing already fell short (its caps note then says so);
  - is **disclosed** otherwise: `cushionShortfall` is set with the cause `envelope` (`envelope+caps` where the sizing fell short as well). Advisory mode, the backup leg (which writes the reserve only) and a reserve already at the write (`reserveAtTarget`: no write to ride) arm nothing; a line within 10 points of full or above a full pool (`nearFull`) and a night whose own expected close is under its line (`closeUnder`) still force-charge.

  The ask and the qualification read one expression (`askOf`). With no eligibility input, as in every caller before this release and every fixture, the plan is v1.199.1's byte for byte.
- **Run to the close.** The armed night keeps the line's ask in its own field (`forceChargeLineAskPct`), beside the cost ceiling, whose meaning is unchanged. Each tick composes the force-charge from the gates as they read at that moment: the cost force-charge (`ARB_OBJECTIVE` cost, `ARB_COST_MAX_SOC_PCT` above 50, a write mode) and the line (any write mode). Where the cost force-charge already stops at the ask + 10 or above, the night runs today's cost force-charge exactly. Otherwise the night runs in **line mode**:
  - the start is timed to reach the start target twenty minutes before the window closes, so a lift of a single point still starts outside the "not worth starting" last twenty minutes. The start target is max(the night's target, the ask + 5), where the night's target is the ask, or the cost ceiling where the cost force-charge rides and is higher. So it is the ask + 5, except on a night whose cost ceiling rides between the ask + 5 and the ask + 10, where it is that ceiling (a cost ceiling of 74 with an ask of 66: 74, not 71). On a line within 10 points of full it is 95, under a 100 ceiling, as on a 90% line;
  - the add-on has no software stop: it switches Charge Now off at the window close (or the on-peak guard, a grid loss, both gates off, a reserve set outside the add-on, or the run backstop). The panel's own stop at its ceiling is below;
  - the panel's force-charge ceiling is at least the ask + 10 (80 for any ask up to 70), at most 100.

  While Charge Now is on under that ceiling, the house and an EV ride the grid, so a load that starts after the start cannot draw the pool under the line. A stop at the ask plus the house's drain to the close was considered first. Emulated, it closed under the line on 197 of 200 nights when a 7 kW EV plugged in 30 minutes before the close.
- **The panel's own stop, and the re-ON.** At its force-charge ceiling the panel switches a slot's Charge Now off by itself and leaves it off. That was read per slot, with the panel-side classifier, on 2026-09-23 (ch1 at 04:19, ch3 at 04:30) and 2026-10-02 (slots 2 and 1 at 04:24–04:25). A start pulled early by a predicted EV can reach the ceiling hours before the close, and a real EV that arrives later then draws the pool back down. Emulated, that closed 8 of 200 year nights under the line with a 20 kWh EV predicted and a 7 kW one arriving 90 minutes out, and up to 65 with larger EVs. On a line night whose ON verified, when the pool reads under the start target (above) and slots of ours read off, those slots are switched back on. The start's own gates apply: the grid known present, healthy vitals, a coherent pool reading, a live slot readback and a live panel ceiling. It happens at most twice a night, 15 minutes apart, and never in the last 20 minutes, so it does not share a slot's command cooldown with the close's OFF. Each re-ON is read back 15 minutes later, as the first ON is; slots still reading off (their Cores under the ceiling) are recorded as not applied, and the next re-ON of the night's two is the retry. With the re-ON, every predicted-EV night of the emulation closes at or above its line, at lines of 70–90% too, on the latch as measured (a slot that reads off). The expected walk models the same latch and re-ON, so the stated close is what the night is expected to reach, not the ceiling.
- **The ceiling the night holds.** The ON records the panel ceiling it read back. A line night that needed no ceiling sync, because the panel already read it, verifies its ON against that ceiling. Before, it used the cost ceiling's value, and a slot refusing the ON at 84% under a 100% ceiling could verify as "at the ceiling". While ON in line mode, a ceiling synced above the line mode's own is lowered to it, once. That happens when the cost gate goes off mid-run on a cost-dominant night, whose cost ceiling (90) would otherwise be the only stop, ten points past the line mode's 80. It lowers only what the panel holds: a live readback inside the panel's 80–100 that reads above the line mode's ceiling. A panel reading at or under it (lowered in the app mid-night) is left alone, because writing the line mode's ceiling there would raise it. Because it only lowers, it is also written under red host vitals, as the OFFs are.
- **The line at risk, while a person can act.** After the ON verified, the re-ON is the add-on's only remedy, and it keys on slots reading off, spends at most two and rides the start's gates. Every other way the line is lost after the start used to show only in the morning: a re-ON refused or not applied, the two spent, the gates holding it, a late load taking the grid cap, or a slot that keeps reading on at its ceiling without importing (unmeasured, below). Now a push goes once a night (`lineAtRiskPushDue`, stamped on the record before it goes) on a night whose record carries a line ask, a cost-dominant one too, with the line gate on. It goes when a fresh, coherent pool reads under the start target (the ask + 5 on a cost-dominant night) and falling against a reading at least 10 minutes older, or flat at or under the ask. Flat with every slot that counts reading on, it waits until the time left no longer covers the charge to the ask at the plan rate, bounded by connected Cores: beside an EV, whole percents read a slow charge as flat. That reading must have been taken at least 15 minutes after the add-on's last ON or re-ON. A re-ON whose readback check found it not applied, or that never reached the panel (every write rate-limited), switched nothing on and does not count as the last ON: the reading is taken against the latest re-ON that did take effect, else the ON, so a night whose re-ONs never take is pushed on the check's own tick. A slot of ours that reads on again after reading off, with no ON write of the add-on's inside its 15-minute readback grace, counts as an ON the same way, and the reading is taken at least 15 minutes after it: a re-ON that took effect only after its check said it had not, a check whose one reading showed every slot off, or Charge Now switched on in the app after the push. So does an ON verified 15 minutes or more after its last attempt. It also needs no OFF of the add-on's yet (on a cost-dominant night that includes its own software stop), more than 20 minutes left before the add-on's close OFF, and no re-ON due. A re-ON whose every write came back rate-limited (the add-on's own per-slot write cooldown) is retried on the first tick at least 15 minutes after it. No push goes while that tick, counted on the minute, falls a full minute before the re-ON's cutoff; otherwise the push goes at once, judged on the hand-back's own tick too, so one on the last tick before the cutoff is pushed there. The hold is judged again every minute, after the once-a-night ceiling lowering too, so on a night whose line stays at risk, short of an OFF of the add-on's, a hand-back gets neither the retry nor the push only when the tick after a hold brings neither before the cutoff: it is lost (a tick that overran or failed, or an add-on restart), reads its clock a whole minute late, has no fresh, coherent panel reading, finds the add-on's writes blocked (two panels on the account, none pinned: a re-ON due is then neither made nor pushed while the block lasts), or is the retry's own tick spent on the ceiling lowering (the retry then waits a tick, as after a lost one). Sent in place of the hold, the push never gives the gates as the reason (once the 15 minutes have run, a retry its gates hold is pushed as any re-ON they hold): it says the switch back on "was refused by its own write cooldown", then, where those 15 minutes end before the cutoff, "it may try once more before ~4:40 AM if the cooldown allows" (the retry may then come, or not), and otherwise "its next try would come after ~4:40 AM, when it no longer switches Charge Now back on". It says where the pool is against the start target and the line, when the window closes and why the add-on cannot act (the two re-ONs spent are said to follow "the panel's own stop" only when that stop is on record), and names the slots; a slot a check found not applied that reads on since is not named as not taking effect. A cost-dominant night has no start target (its start is timed to its cost target), so there the threshold is named by its margin ("under 75%, 5.6 points above tonight's 69.4% outage line") and the reason is that its force-charge is the cost plan's, with its own stop, and is not switched back on for the line. The slots: reading off, "Charge Now switched on for slot(s) … in the EcoFlow app can still lift the pool (the add-on switches it off at the close)"; reading on while the pool is not rising, "A large load such as an EV shares the grid input: stopping it may let the pool rise. With none on, switching Charge Now off and on again in the EcoFlow app may restart the charge." No write rests on the unmeasured shape. When that shape is seen (slots of ours reading on, their Cores under the ceiling, the pool falling against a reading taken at least 15 minutes after the last ON or re-ON), one warning is logged a night, push or not, kept on the record so a restart does not log it again. Emulated, it is never logged on the latch as measured, an ON that took effect late or one a person switched on (below).
- **Worked example** (2027-01-04 21:30 at 0.944, supervised). The pool should open the 23:00–05:00 window near 50.8%. The reserve is set to 50%. The force-charge for the 55.55% line is expected to switch on at ~03:25 and run to the close, taking the pool to ~78.4%, ~28.2 kWh expected. Before, nothing was expected to charge and the pool closed near 50%. The rationale says: "The reserve is set to 50%, the most the panel accepts as a backup reserve; tonight's 55.55% outage line is above it, so the panel's force-charge switches on at ~03:25 and stays on until the window closes, taking the pool to ~78.4%. After the window the reserve returns to 30% and the house can draw the pool down as usual." In advisory mode the same night says the line is above the 50% reserve "and no force-charge runs in advisory mode, so the cushion is not fully held — residual risk remains." A night whose expected walk reaches the ceiling is worded by the panel's stop: 2026-10-29 21:30 at 0.944 (line 65.95%, an EV predicted) says the force-charge "switches on at ~23:00 and runs until the panel switches it off at its 80% ceiling; the add-on switches it back on if the pool falls under 71% before the window closes (at most twice), ~72.1% expected at the close."
- **One level on every surface.** The rationale's headline, the evening push's title and its force-charge clause, the spoken notice and the card all state the expected close of the line force-charge's walk. The spoken notice adds "The panel keeps only 50 percent in reserve." The line sentence appears only on a night that runs in line mode, never on a night whose cost force-charge already clears it. A line night whose pool is expected to stay at or above the panel ceiling says the force-charge is armed for the line "but is not expected to start". It never says the force-charge stays on to the close, and names no "force-charge to" its expected close. A night whose expected walk reaches the panel ceiling (`expectedLineCeilingStop`, 6 of the year's 39 line-mode plans) says on the rationale, the push, the card and the 21:30 log that the panel switches it off at its ceiling and the add-on switches it back on under the start target before the close (at most twice). It never says the force-charge stays on until the window closes, and never calls the ceiling a backstop. The "charge/pool caps" note, and the "no EVSE prediction" note beside it, appear only where the sizing itself fell short. An envelope disclosure names the envelope on the push, the card, the mid-day push and the shadow's log. With nothing armed they say why (advisory mode, the backup leg). An armed night keeps its line clause and adds, by one predicate (`lineNotHeldOf`), a NOTE where its walk closes under the line ("expected to close at ~57.3%, under tonight's 68.85% outage line", 2027-08-09 21:30 at 0.944, 13 h), where the line is above a full pool, or where a near-full line holds without its usual margin; the card (in the warning colour) and the 21:30 log ("Line NOT held") say the same, on a carried night whose sizing fell short too. No armed night is told "no force-charge". The line-mode ON log names the panel's own stop and the re-ON, not a backstop. The overnight log's line-mode reasons for not starting yet name the line by its own number, not its ask, and never say the force-charge stays on to the close; "already at the panel ceiling" says the ceiling is above the line, at it, or that the line is above a full pool, by their order. A line named beside the 50% reserve, a full pool, the ceiling or a pool reading is given to two decimals where one would read as it (50.03%, 99.96%), and a line stated at 50.00 and asked at 51 (2026-11-25 on nameplate at 11 h) is not numbered beside the reserve on the push, the card and the 21:30 log; the rationale there still reads "tonight's 50% outage line is above it". The ON, re-ON, ceiling-lowering, at-risk and declined-start log lines name the line to one decimal, not the record's two.
- **At run time.** A night whose record carries a line ask, a cost-dominant one too, pushes when anything but the clock or the pool holds its start. Causes: the ceiling refused or unread, Charge Now already on, the grid unknown or absent, red vitals, no slot read back or connected, an incoherent SoC, or the write block refusing the ON or the ceiling sync. Each pushes once a night after 10 minutes unbroken, recorded first so a restart does not repeat it. The push's latest start errs early on every input, so can precede the timed one: ⌈L⌉ by the close, from at most the reserve, at the lower of the live and slot-bounded plan rates, with the EV's energy, e.g. "If it has not started by ~04:24, the pool may close under the line; if it never starts, the add-on adds nothing above the 50% reserve." It can run late at a stale panel's plan rate on a plant under ~10 kW, or with an EV above its prediction or a house rising after the push. A hand Charge Now stops at the panel's ceiling with no re-ON: an EV to the close can still take the pool under the line. A time gone by the push is not quoted (13% of the walked year's pushes, up from 4%): "The later it starts, the lower the pool may close, perhaps under the line". An ON that took on no slot says it "has not taken effect: unless it applies late, the add-on adds nothing above the 50% reserve", and that the pool may close under. Neither push can know where the pool will close (it may read above the line, a declined cause may be a person's Charge Now, an islanded pool drains through the reserve), and a late ON or reserve write is still verified. A line of 50.00, which the reserve holds, is never said to close under. One that failed on some slots says the pool may close short of it. Both say what can still lift the pool (Charge Now on for those slots in the app, switched off at the close), never "no action needed", on a cost-dominant night too. A reserve write never read back on such a night says that until the panel reads 50% it keeps its normal reserve, under the line, and, while a start can come, that the force-charge for the line waits on that write: "To let it start, set the backup reserve to 50% in the EcoFlow app now … No force-charge starts after ~4:40 AM, and one that late may close under the line." Elsewhere (a cancelled night, 20 minutes or less to the close) it and its logs say why none starts. A write missing its 30-minute apply window (red vitals, a stale reading, a restart, a reserve raised in the app, a cloud refusing it) pushes too: an attempt in those words (no reading: only the wait); with none issued a reading starts nothing, so it names Charge Now by hand. So does a reserve set outside the add-on: the force-charge stops or does not start, and setting it back does not undo that. So does a `gridLoss` OFF no abort reports (a gridSta reading with grid flow measured), and a charge plan's window no record holds with no evening job left (`noArm`). A recompute of a run a cancel or an OFF ended, of an ON warned of on any slot, of every slot off with no re-ON left, or such a window, claims no ON (`startClosed`), and with no verified write standing, or a partial ON, the card leads with that; one still on says so and, with none left, drops the re-ON clause. Their titles and logs say "has not taken effect"; the held start's, "cannot start yet". A night with no line ask keeps v1.199.1's words, pinned, but for the armed banner at a reserve reading the target (none is due) and one log line's "no live slot readback".
- **The morning account** of a night whose record carries a line ask says the force-charge "ran to the window close for the 55.6% outage line" only when its OFF was the window's. After any other OFF it says when and why ("ran … until 3:30am (switched off: the force-charge was disabled by the options)"); when the panel's own stop ended it, "until the panel's own stop switched Charge Now off at 3:10am". It names the re-ONs, and those a readback check found still off; it says "after the panel's own stop" only when that stop is on record. A re-ON counts as resuming the charge only when its readback check found a slot that took it. The panel's stop ended the charge unless a re-ON after it resumed the charge, any of them and not only the last: the record keeps the latest re-ON that took effect (`forceChargeLineReOnAppliedAtMs`), across the next re-ON and a restart. A slot of ours seen on again after the stop (a re-ON applied late, Charge Now switched on in the app) resumed it too, and a stop after that (the panel switching it off again at its ceiling) is a stop of its own, the one the account names. So when the re-ONs after the stop all failed, or never reached the panel, the account says the panel's stop ended the charge. When the last re-ON took on no slot (its check found every slot it switched still off, or it never reached the panel), the account does not say "ran to the window close", whatever an earlier re-ON did: a re-ON is only written to slots reading off, so those slots read off before the close. It says "Charge Now read off on slot(s) 1, 2, 3 before the window close and was switched back on 2 time(s) (slot(s) still off at the readback check after 2 of them)", naming the slots the last re-ON's check found off (none for one that never reached the panel), with "after the panel's own stop" when that stop is on record. A slot of ours seen on again after the last re-ON leaves "ran to the window close". Where the pool read at the close is given, with "under the 55.6% outage line" when it closed under; with no fresh reading it says "the pool was not read at the close", never silence; over 15 minutes after the close, what it reads now, no verdict. A write never read back is told so, never "raised", on the banner too. A grid loss or a cancel gives where it reads then against the line; a night with nothing to restore (no write, a reserve set outside the add-on) has its own account at the close. A night with no line ask reads as v1.199.1, word for word. The 21:30 log says "Force-charge: LINE — …".
- **The morning refresh** of a mid-day leg lowers or drops the cost ceiling only. A leg's line ask rides through unchanged.
- **The mid-day re-entry hold** never holds a leg the line force-charge is armed on. The hold writes nothing and runs no force-charge, so holding such a leg would leave the pool where it stands against an outage line above the 50% reserve; and on a line-mode leg the level is the walk's close capped at what the window can reach, not a write's target, so more cushion would turn an armed leg into a hold. Such a leg charges, is judged against its line as any armed night is, and its rationale says the re-entry line is not met. With a 31% floor and a 5 kW window, a 61.24% line at 8 h of cushion charges toward ~55.1%, under the 56% re-entry line (`lineForceCharge.test.ts` T23); after the restore the panel may then hold the pool while the house buys from the grid into on-peak. The mid-day push names Charge Now on every leg the line force-charge is armed on, also where the stated level is the 50% reserve (the window's reach, or no start expected): those legs are still armed, and Charge Now switches on just in time. A leg expected to draw nothing says the reserve is held at 50% "and Charge Now is armed for the 60% outage line (not expected to start)" (the pool at 90% at 21:30, a 60.03% line): the line as the overnight clause names it, not its ask of 61. The mid-day leg runs in shadow only.
- **Ledger.** Plan columns `cushion_line_force_charge_pct` (the ask armed) and `cushion_envelope_shortfall` (1/0), written on every plan row. The actuation column `force_charge_mode` ('cost' or 'line', the mode at the ON). A carried night whose sizing held its line and that closes under it is a strike, counted in the readiness gate's new `lineForceChargeStrikes` diagnostic. A row whose plan disclosed a shortfall (a disclosed line, or a carried night with the caps note) is exempt like any disclosed shortfall, except an armed one whose force-charge never switched on and that closed under its line (or under 100% where the line is above it): a strike, an owner's cancel after the apply too (the ledger records no cancel). An ON sent that never took leaves such a row exempt; it is pushed at run time. `strikesMeasurable` counts an armed, actuated row of that kind as one the strike rule grades, so a 0 beside such rows reads as none found, not as "cannot count". A close exactly at its line less 0.05 now reads as held, as the rule states, in the plan's carry test, on the card and in the scorer's close verdict (`realizedCushionBreach`): computed in binary, 50.35 − 0.05 came out above 50.3, so 128 of the 501 one-decimal closes from 50% to 100% read as under a line 0.05 above them. A live row moves only where its close reading is exactly its line less 0.05.

**Unchanged**

- Every sizing and worst-case figure: the written reserve, `buyKwh`, the de-biased buy, `requiredExtraKwh`, `targetSocPct`, `setpointSocPct` and the cost ceiling equal v1.199.1's for the same inputs, on every plan of the year at both scales. `cushionTroughSocPct` stays the sized trough.
- The cost force-charge on every night without a line above 50: its target, start, stop and panel ceiling. A record armed before this release decides exactly as before.
- The live plant at today's settings: its line is about 31–33% (31.13% on nameplate, 32.02% once measured), so it never crosses 50 and no plan, entity or ledger figure moves.
- `CURRENT_ALGO_VERSION` stays 3. No scored column's formula or sign changes, and `cushion_shortfall` still means "the plan disclosed it cannot hold its line", with a second cause. The year of overnight plans in `legacyIdentity.golden.json`, the overnight text goldens and the other digests are unchanged. No option is added.

**Limits**

- **Charge Now during an outage is not established.** On a line night in `supervised` or `auto` mode, Charge Now is on from its timed start to the window close (or to the panel's own stop at its ceiling, and again after a re-ON), whatever `ARB_OBJECTIVE` says. A resilience-objective plant therefore gets Charge Now for the first time. Emulated over the year's line shapes (not measured), a slot reads Charge Now on for 74 minutes on average, 142 minutes for lines of 70–90%, and 138 minutes on the year's lines with a 20 kWh EV predicted. Those figures count the panel's own OFF at its ceiling. A line within 10 points of full adds 182 minutes on average (224 and 335 with a predicted EV). The panel's own stop at a 100% ceiling has not been observed (only at 80 and 90%; observe one before running lines above 90%): a slot that kept reading on there would close all 120 emulated near-full nights with a 20 kWh EV predicted under the line (each pushed). No vendor text says how a slot with Charge Now on behaves when the grid fails, and no outage has overlapped Charge Now on the reference plant. Before a plant with a line above 50% runs in a write mode, an attended test is recommended: Charge Now on for one slot, the main breaker opened, the backed-up loads confirmed up and the pack confirmed discharging.
- **The off switches.** `ARB_COST_MAX_SOC_PCT` at 50 now stops the cost force-charge only; it no longer stops every Charge Now. A line night is stopped by `NIGHT_CHARGE_MODE` `advisory`, by the per-night cancel, by a reserve set in the app, or by a cushion whose line is at or under 50% (fewer cushion hours, a lower floor).
- **The overshoot.** Running to the close buys the lead the start is timed with. The emulated mean close sits about 13 points above the ask (≈ 20 kWh into the pack against ≈ 8 kWh for a stop at the line). It is overnight energy stored rather than lost, and the close is bounded by the panel ceiling. So on a line night the expected buy usually **exceeds the worst-case figure the plan is sized on**, whether or not an EV is predicted. On the year 24 of the 39 line-mode plans exceed it, 20 exceed the de-biased figure, and 12 of the 20 with no predicted EV exceed it. For example, 2026-10-29 21:30 at 0.944 expects ~49.7 kWh against a 49.6 kWh worst case. A predicted EV pulls its start to the window open, and the pool reaches the 80% ceiling and is drawn back to ~72.1% by the close.
- **A late unpredicted load.** The start is timed on the charge rate as it reads then. A large load that the plan did not predict and that starts after the force-charge takes the grid cap the pack was timed on. The pack then arrives short and the night closes under its line, a strike. Emulated over the year's 200 nights (rounded readings, then floored):
  - an 11.52 kW EV (the default `EV_MAX_LOAD_W`) from 90 minutes before the close: 73 and 82;
  - the same EV from 120 minutes: 55 and 61;
  - a 7 kW EV from 120 minutes: 5 and 8;
  - an ON 5 minutes late with a 7 kW EV from 90 minutes: 1 and 4.

  A pack taking 85% of the rate with that EV closes 1 and 3 of 50 under. A **predicted** EV is carried (above).
- **Cost-dominant nights keep the cost force-charge.** A night whose cost stop is at the ask + 10 or above runs today's cost force-charge, software stop included, and a late EV after that stop can take a line of 70–90% under: 5 of 140 emulated nights with a 7 kW EV from 60 minutes, 15 with an 11.52 kW one. No year-shaped line (at most 65.95%) did. The line-at-risk push goes on these nights only before that stop: an OFF of the add-on's ends it, so a late EV that takes the pool under after the stop is not pushed. Over the emulated cost-dominant cells (the ask + 10 with a 7 kW EV from 60 or 90 minutes or an 11.52 kW one from 60, and the ask + 15 with the 11.52 kW one; rounded and floored readings), 89 nights close under. The push reached 30 of them, each one that ended with no OFF of the add-on's: none of the 17 with a 7 kW EV; with an 11.52 kW one, 5 of 15 and 16 of 27 floored at the ask + 10, and 5 of 15 and 4 of 15 floored at the ask + 15. Of the 59 it did not reach, 49 ended after the cost stop. The other 10 had no OFF of the add-on's: slow packs still rising at the close (7 floored with the 7 kW EV from 90 minutes, 3 floored with the 11.52 kW one), the load-that-slows-the-charge residual below.
- **What the re-ON reads, and what was measured.** It keys on the slots reading off, as the panel's own ceiling stop read per slot on 2026-09-23 and 2026-10-02. 2026-09-18, cited in the 1.169.0 notes as "with Charge Now still on", measured the 90% ceiling reached at 03:30, grid import at 0 W and the pool drawn from 90% to 86% by 05:00. That night's slot state is the add-on's own record (no OFF was sent before 05:00), not a per-slot readback, and the drift watch of the time filed a panel-side OFF as the add-on's own write. So it is consistent with the latch as measured and does not establish another shape. A slot an operator switches off in the app reads the same, and is switched back on within the night's two; the off switches of a line night are above. A panel that refuses the ceiling lowered mid-run keeps the cost ceiling as its stop: the pre-fix overshoot, an economic matter.
- **A slot that keeps reading on at its ceiling (unmeasured).** If a slot stopped importing at its ceiling but kept reading Charge Now on, nothing of the add-on's would switch it back on, and a predicted-EV night that meets a late EV would close under its line. Emulated (`lineForceChargeClose.test.ts`, pinned as known failures, not a measurement), per predicted-EV cell: on the year's lines 0 to 65 of 200 nights with rounded readings and 0 to 72 floored (8 with a 20 kWh EV predicted and a 7 kW one from 90 minutes; 44 with 30 kWh and an 11 kW one); at lines of 70–90%, 52 to 140 of 140. An early EV session then a late load spent the push before the load on 10,429 of 14,069 nights under; now 16. On the latch as measured the push goes on none of the 3,740 emulated predicted-EV nights: 2 and 5 floored before a flat pool waited on the time left. With an EV session that ends before the close (32,000 year nights, 7.68 to 11.52 kW) it went on 9,732 that hold, a median 209 minutes out, and goes on 100, every EV unpredicted. The wait costs reach: of 698 pushes on the late unpredicted loads' nights under, 2 go (each closing 0.1 under) and 158 come a median of 20 minutes later. On a wider grid 22 of 6,942 go, the deepest closing 4 points under: each a 4.5-4.8 kW pack, the pool rising at least 7 points after the reading. A plant delivering under the plan rate (a grid cap under about 12 kW, or a steady 7-10 kW house) is pushed later, and a restart there may no longer reach the line. It reaches 69 of the 73 year nights under with an 11.52 kW EV from 90 minutes (70 of 82 floored). A load that slows the charge without stopping it (a 7 kW EV from 120 minutes; an ON 5 minutes late with a 7 kW EV from 90) leaves the pool rising, and is not pushed.
- **A re-ON that is accepted and never takes effect (unmeasured).** The cloud accepts the write and the slots still read off at its readback check 15 minutes later. That shape has not been seen on the reference plant; the add-on handles it with that check, the push's "has not taken effect" cause and the morning account. Emulated (`lineForceChargeClose.test.ts`, variant `reOnInert`, pinned per cell), the predicted-EV nights then close under as on the unmeasured latch above: 390 of the 4,400 year-shaped nights with rounded and floored readings, and 2,615 of 3,080 at lines of 70–90%. - **A line within 10 points of full is force-charged and disclosed.** Its start is timed to 95% under the panel's 100% ceiling, so the margin above the line shrinks to nothing near 100%. - **The stated close is what the window can deliver.** The expected walk books the reserve leg as instant; where the two ask more than the window can deliver, the close is capped at the window-open pool plus that deliverable and the window's P50 sun, and the panel's stop is named only where that reach clears the ceiling. 2026-10-29 21:30 on nameplate at 14 h states ~92.8% (30% at the open, ~57.9 kWh delivered), not the walk's ~98.4% at its 100% ceiling. Their stated start is the walk's, from the reserve; the tick, timing on the lower pool it reads, can only start sooner (inferred).
- **More cushion never switches the force-charge off, and never lowers its panel ceiling or, with `ARB_COST_MAX_SOC_PCT` at 95 or less, its start target; its expected close and buy can still fall.** On the year at 4–20 h, in one-hour steps, the close falls on 384 steps on nameplate and 395 at 0.944, and the buy on 136 and 138. The buy falls by up to 9.25 and 9.24 kWh (2026-10-13 13:00 on nameplate, 10 → 11 h: 30.72 → 21.47 kWh, with the caps note). None falls to an undisclosed close under the line, and the mean expected buy per armed or disclosed plan rises from 28.89 kWh at 8 h to 37.66 at 20 h (29.55 to 38.04), not at every step. With a cost maximum above 95, a line crossing 90% moves the start from the cost ceiling to 95% (14 and 19 steps with the year's cost cases at 100); the ceiling stays 100.
- **Run-time refusals are strikes.** A carried night whose sizing held its line and that cannot run (the ceiling refused, Charge Now already on, the grid unknown, red vitals, no slot connected, an ON that never took) closes near 50% under its line and remains an engine-fault strike, fail-closed. So is a night whose write never held its armed line (`actuated` 0, a missed apply). An armed night whose plan disclosed a shortfall (its line, or the caps note) is a strike only when its force-charge never switched on; an ON that never took, a slow pack or a late load leaves it exempt. The declined-start push, the line-at-risk push and `lineForceChargeStrikes` make it legible.
- **A panel reading 50% at the evening plan gets no force-charge.** With no write it has nothing to ride, so such a night discloses its line; a reserve lowered later can get its write, with none for the line. Riding a standing reserve would add an ON: not done.
- **Slow charge at high asks.** For lines of 70–90% a slow pack can arrive after the close. Out of 35 nights with the pack taking 70% of the rate, 8 close under the line, and 30 at 60%. With an EV from 90 minutes out it is 1 of 140. With whole-percent readings floored it is 11, 33 and 10.
- The expected figure is not monotone in the target: a higher target starts earlier from a less-drained pool. With eligibility, at the fixture's own cushion hours, it never falls below v1.199.1's on the year, and that is pinned rather than assumed.
- Lines above 50% on the legacy-pct basis and the backup leg's walk are not this change. Those setpoints are whole-house walks, not a line at the close.
