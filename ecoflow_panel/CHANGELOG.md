## 1.205.1

### A refused night says what nothing is armed to hold

**Night charge**

- **A refused night with no line force-charge.** When the arm is refused, the 21:30 notice says the add-on will not charge (or will not raise the reserve) tonight. A plan with a line force-charge then said that nothing is armed to carry its line (1.205.0). A plan with no line force-charge and no envelope note, whose cushion still asked for energy, said nothing more. Example: 2026-10-13 21:30 on nameplate at 4 hours asked 15.19 kWh against a 31.3% line over a 16% floor, and the notice ended "The add-on will not charge tonight." That night's notice now ends "NOTE: nothing is armed to hold tonight's 31.3% floor+cushion line — residual risk remains." The line is named as the armed notice names it ("the floor+cushion line is 31.3%"). Where the sizing fell short, the caps note comes first. The reserve-hold shape gets the same note. On the fixture year's 21:30 plans at 4–20 hours on both pool bases, this adds the note to 3,711 charge-shape and 1,128 hold-shape refused notices per write mode (999 and 136 of them after the caps note). Unchanged: a plan whose envelope note fires keeps that note only, the 727 refused plans whose cushion asked for nothing read as before, and the 4,094 refused line nights keep 1.205.0's note.
- **The mid-day notice too.** A refused mid-day cycle whose cost leg has no line ask and no envelope note, and whose cushion asked for energy, says "NOTE: nothing is armed to hold this leg's 31.3% floor+cushion line — residual risk remains." This note comes before the backup leg's note. The shadow record carries the line for this (`cushionAskedLinePct`, optional). A record from before 1.205.1 reads as before.
- **A refused cushion-only night.** With the Cores dark (0 scored calibration days), the night plan is the cushion-only one, and its armed notice names the line in its basis sentence: "the 33.3% line is the 16% reserve floor plus 4 h of islanded backup load at the window close". The refused notice states only the short basis ("Outage cushion only; no solar basis (…)."), which does not define that line, so its note keeps "floor+cushion line", on the same figure. Every note of a refused cushion-only notice now follows its basis sentence, with the caps note first, as the armed notice orders them. Before, its notes came between the lead and the basis. Where the panel's own readings were refused (the 7-day balance above its threshold), the panel's sentence stays in the basis, before every note, as on the armed notice. Example: 2026-10-12 with the Cores dark, on the held ratio at 4 hours, is a reserve hold. Its refused notice now ends "The add-on will not raise the reserve tonight. Outage cushion only; no solar basis (only 0 scored calibration day(s), …). NOTE: nothing is armed to hold tonight's 33.3% floor+cushion line — residual risk remains." On the fixture year with the Cores dark there are 614 cushion-only charge plans per write mode. Of these, 467 carry the unheld-line note alone and 65 carry it after the caps note. 61 carry a line night's refused note, 21 of them after the caps note. 21 carry no note and read as before.
- **The mid-day backup leg's note follows its own line.** The mid-day notice's "the outage line is above the 50% the panel accepts as a reserve and no force-charge carries it on the backup leg" always took the 50% form, because the backup leg carried no line figure. A backup leg sized on a line at or below 50 could therefore read the 50% form beside a cost leg that named the 49% the panel holds. Example: a backup leg sized on 2026-11-27 21:30 at ×0.944 and 16 hours, a 49.99% line. The note is now worded by the named leg's own line, as the overnight envelope note is: "above the 49% the panel holds the pool at under the 50% it accepts as a reserve". The same applies to a cost leg with no line ask and to the shadow's evening log line for the backup leg. Each leg's note follows that leg's line, never the other leg's. Only a line at or below 50 changes: 49.00 to 50.00 on the fixture year's plans, 49.00 included (2027-08-31 21:30 at ×0.944 and 7 hours, as that night's overnight notice already words it). Above 50, or with no figure, the words are unchanged byte for byte.
- **A refused mid-day cycle gets no morning refresh.** The shadow's 09:30 refresh checked only that the cost leg had a complete basis and charged. It did not check whether the cycle would arm. A cycle whose 21:30 preview said "The add-on will not charge tomorrow" was re-sized like any other. In the fixture's Wednesday 2026-10-14 cycle, refused because a prior night's reserve write is unresolved and re-sized on a 60% pool, the log read "a live refresh would push this". Its preview read "Charge plan updated: still ~89.5% today 10am–3pm, now about 9 kWh … Cancel from the charge card before 10:00 AM." A refused cycle is no longer re-sized. From 30 minutes before the open its refresh is recorded as `not-armed`, with no push and the reason "the evening cycle would not arm (…), so there was nothing to refresh". The record shows on the ledger's `shadow_refresh_disposition` and `shadow_refreshed_*` columns, on `midday_charge_refresh`, in the log and on the card. The ledger columns and the figure entities keep the evening's figures beside that disposition, but no word says they stand, because nothing was armed: the log line states no figures for it, and the card's row reads "Morning refresh 09:30: none — the evening cycle would not arm (…), so there was nothing to refresh". Before the record is made, from the evening, the card's row reads "none" rather than "due" and `midday_charge_refresh` reads `not-armed` rather than `pending`, in either write mode. The record survives a restart. A record persisted by 1.205.0 still reads. An armed or advisory cycle refreshes exactly as before.
- **Armed output is unchanged but for one correction.** Every armed and advisory 21:30 notice, every armed mid-day notice and spoken notice, the card and the plan rationale stay byte-identical, with one exception: the mid-day "no force-charge carries it" note and the shadow's evening log line for the backup leg, for a leg whose line is at or below 50. On the fixture year's 21:30 plans at 4–20 hours on both pools, with the line gate on and off, with the night's own backup leg and without, in the three modes, that moves 2,430 armed renders in 1,458 records (486 configurations), every one naming a line from 49.00 to 50.00. With the correction undone, all 459,170 renders of that sweep hash to 1.205.0's. No planner figure moves, and the plan-identity and walk-identity fixtures are unchanged.
- **Not covered:** a downgrade to 1.205.0 after a `not-armed` refresh is recorded, before the window closes, re-decides that cycle as 1.205.0 did: 1.205.0 does not know the disposition, so it drops the refresh, re-sizes the cycle (up to 5 minutes before the open) or records it `stood` (after), and overwrites the ledger's refresh columns (`shadow_refresh_disposition`, `shadow_refresh_weather_age_s` and `shadow_refreshed_*`).

## 1.205.0

### A line night is claimed only where it holds with an allowance for a late switch-on at the window open

**Night charge**

- **Why.** A force-charge timed to start at the window open cannot run at the open. The reserve write applies on the first tick at or after it; the ON waits for that write's readback and for the panel ceiling's; a Core that does not take the ON is re-issued only after its readback grace. On 2026-11-10 the ON went 2:47 after the open and every Core read ON 19:47 after it. A start the plan times inside the window carries its own slack (⌈line⌉ + 5 by the close less 20 minutes); a start at the open carried none, and the plan claimed the line on a walk that charged from the first minute.
- **The claim is two walks.** A night the line force-charge is armed on (in line mode, or carried by the cost force-charge with its walk past the stop) is claimed only where two walks hold its outage line (at or above the line less 0.05, the scorer's tolerance): the median walk, as before, and the same walk with nothing charging before each whole minute up to the window open plus 20 minutes, its reach counted from that minute — the lowest close of them, never floored at the 50% reserve. The 20 minutes are the actuator's own: the ON's 15-minute readback grace and five 60-second ticks (the write's tick, its readback, the ceiling's readback with the ON, a tick of stamp jitter before the re-issue, and the re-issue read back). The walk is not monotone in the minute (a later ON can latch at the ceiling later and close higher), so every minute is walked.
- **Otherwise disclosed, still armed.** Such a night is disclosed with a new reason, `lateStart`: the cushion is not fully met, cause `envelope`, the force-charge still armed and run exactly as before. Its stated level, kWh, start time, push title and the ledger's expected buy stay the median walk's. Nothing written, timed or started changes.
- **The words.** Where the plan's own force-charge start is inside the allowance (`cushionLateStartAtOpen`), the line is said to be too close to the expected close "to allow for a late switch-on at the window open"; where the start is past it or none is expected, "to allow for a late start of the window's charge" (4 of the 57 nights below), never a switch-on the plan does not make. The rationale and the 21:30 notice: "on the median load the pool is expected to close at ~X%, too close to tonight's L% outage line to allow for …". The ARMED log line: "Line NOT assured: ~X% expected at the close, too close to the line to allow for …", never "NOT held". The card, on a line-mode night: "too close to the line to allow for …; residual risk remains"; on a cost-carried night its row says the force-charge runs and the line is not claimed. The mid-day notice has its own sentence, and the mid-day record keeps the reason and its wording across a restart. The spoken notice already says the cushion is not fully met.
- **A cost-carried night names its close, not its stop.** Its close past the stop is capped at what the window can reach, as a line walk's close is (the window-open pool, what the window can deliver on the median load, and its median sun): 2027-02-05 at ×0.944 and 20 hours named ~65.8% from a pool the window can take to ~59.9%. On a cost-carried `lateStart` night the plan now carries that close (`expectedCloseAfterStopPct`, before only on a night closing under its line), and the rationale, the 21:30 notice and the ARMED line name it ("~59.9% … too close to tonight's 58.9% outage line"), never the 87% stop. The stop, the buy and every write are unchanged. On the fixture year's 21:30 plans at 4–20 hours on both pools no night's claim moves by the cap; at 270 W standby one does (2027-02-19 at ×0.944, 10 hours), now disclosed.
- **Mid-window.** The allowance is anchored at the window open: a recompute walks only what is left of it, and it lapses 20 minutes after the open whatever is read. A verified ON on every Core of the night ends it — only when stamped at or after the window open and at or before the pool reading the plan walks from (the time and the pool are read before the recompute's first wait and the stamp after it, so a verify landing in between keeps the allowance for that one recompute, the conservative side; nothing is pushed). The plan the evening job arms from never reads it.
- **A line in (49, 50].** Judged on the unfloored walk, not on the 50% the stated level is floored at: 2026-11-27 21:30 at ×0.944 and 16 hours, a 49.99% line the median walk holds (capped at a reach of ~50.0%) and the walk from the allowance's end does not (~47.5%), is disclosed; floored, every minute would read 50% and the night be carried.
- **Which nights.** At the 4-hour cushion no line on the fixture year is above 50%, so no night changes. With more cushion hours, 57 of the fixture year's 21:30 plans at 4–20 hours on both pool bases are disclosed (45 in line mode, 12 cost-carried) on 22 dates, 53 of them one-hour windows (49 on a Friday). Replayed through the real reserve, ceiling and ON decisions, with every Core switching on and with one or two Cores 17 minutes late, at the fixture's grid and at 19.1 kW, no claimed one-hour line night closes under its line (on 1.204.0, 7 to 17 of 168 did).
- **Not covered, and not pushed:** a start worse than every Core switching on 20 minutes after the open (a ceiling re-sync beside a late Core, a re-ON that takes late), and an unpredicted load. A Core that never takes the ON is still pushed, as is a refused ceiling. After the allowance lapses, a recompute of a line in (49, 50.05] still reads the reserve-floored close. A close the walk ends on the written 50% reserve is still read at 50%, so a line in (49, 50.05] whose walk draws the pool back down to the reserve is carried (2026-10-19 21:30 on nameplate at 13 hours, a 49.63% line); a pool drawn down onto a written reserve is measured held at it on every hold under 7 hours, and three holds longer than 11.8 hours read a point under it.

### A refused arm says once that nothing is armed to carry the line

- When the arm is refused, the 21:30 notice says the add-on will not charge (or will not raise the reserve) tonight, and then appended the armed night's line note: a close above or near the line that only the unarmed charge would make (2027-04-09 at ×0.944 and 7 hours: "expected to close at ~59.5%, too close to tonight's 59.4% outage line" from a pool opening at ~51.8%). The same held for a night closing under its line, near full or above full, and the mid-day notice did the same. On a plan with a line force-charge both notices now say once that the line is above the 50% the panel accepts as a reserve (beside the 49% it holds, for a line in (49, 50]) and nothing is armed to carry it — residual risk remains. The caps note, every other plan's note and every armed shape are unchanged; the card already drops its line row under a refused arm.
- Under a refused arm the card's worst-case stat no longer says "reserve set to N%" (2026-10-13 at ×0.944 and 5 hours said "reserve set to 38%" while the reserve stayed at the 16% floor); it names no reserve. Armed nights keep the label.

### Measured: how the panel holds a pool it draws down onto a reserve

- Of 34 holds from 2026-08-30 to 2026-11-09 where the pool was drawn down onto a written reserve, every Core's discharge stopped at once when the lowest slot read the reserve; the pool then read the reserve or a point above it, and no hold under 7 hours read below it. Three of the seven holds longer than 11.8 hours read a point under it 7.3–9.2 hours after the stop, the slots settling 0.03–0.14 points an hour with the Cores at 0 W and no top-up. Only two were onto a 50% reserve, both written onto a pool already near it. A charge-up to a 50% reserve still holds at 49. Recorded in the planning reference; no figure the planner uses changes.
