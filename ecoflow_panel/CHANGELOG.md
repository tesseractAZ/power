## 1.205.0

### A line night is claimed only where it holds with an allowance for a late switch-on at the window open

**Night charge**

- **Why.** A force-charge timed to start at the window open cannot run at the open. The reserve write applies on the first tick at or after it; the ON waits for that write's readback and for the panel ceiling's; a Core that does not take the ON is re-issued only after its readback grace. On 2026-11-10 the ON went 2:47 after the open and every Core read ON 19:47 after it. A start the plan times inside the window carries its own slack (⌈line⌉ + 5 by the close less 20 minutes); a start at the open carried none, and the plan claimed the line on a walk that charged from the first minute.
- **The claim is two walks.** A night the line force-charge is armed on (in line mode, or carried by the cost force-charge with its walk past the stop) is claimed only where two walks hold its outage line (at or above the line less 0.05, the scorer's tolerance): the median walk, as before, and the same walk with nothing charging before each whole minute up to the window open plus 20 minutes, its reach counted from that minute — the lowest close of them, never floored at the 50% reserve. The 20 minutes are the actuator's own: the ON's 15-minute readback grace and five 60-second ticks (the write's tick, its readback, the ceiling's readback with the ON, a tick of stamp jitter before the re-issue, and the re-issue read back). The walk is not monotone in the minute (a later ON can latch at the ceiling later and close higher), so every minute is walked.
- **Otherwise disclosed, still armed.** Such a night is disclosed with a new reason, `lateStart`: the cushion is not fully met, cause `envelope`, the force-charge still armed and run exactly as before. Its stated level, kWh, start time, push title and the ledger's expected buy stay the median walk's. Nothing written, timed or started changes.
- **The words.** Where the plan's own force-charge start is inside the allowance (`cushionLateStartAtOpen`), the line is said to be too close to the expected close "to allow for a late switch-on at the window open"; where the start is past it or none is expected, "to allow for a late start of the window's charge" (4 of the 57 nights below), never a switch-on the plan does not make. The rationale and the 21:30 notice: "on the median load the pool is expected to close at ~X%, too close to tonight's L% outage line to allow for …". The ARMED log line: "Line NOT assured: ~X% expected at the close, too close to the line to allow for …", never "NOT held". The card, on a line-mode night: "too close to the line to allow for …; residual risk remains"; on a cost-carried night its row says the force-charge runs and the line is not claimed. The mid-day notice has its own sentence, and the mid-day record keeps the reason and its wording across a restart. The spoken notice already says the cushion is not fully met.
- **A cost-carried night names its close, not its stop.** Its close past the stop is capped at what the window can reach, as a line walk's close is (the window-open pool, what the window can deliver on the median load, and its median sun): 2027-02-05 at ×0.944 and 20 hours named ~65.8% from a pool the window can take to ~59.9%. On a cost-carried `lateStart` night the plan now carries that close (`expectedCloseAfterStopPct`, before only on a night closing under its line), and the rationale, the 21:30 notice and the ARMED line name it ("~59.9% … too close to tonight's 58.9% outage line"), never the 87% stop. The stop, the buy and every write are unchanged. On the fixture year's 21:30 plans at 4–20 hours on both pools no night's claim moves by the cap; at 270 W standby one does (2027-02-19 at ×0.944, 10 hours), now disclosed.
- **Mid-window.** The allowance is anchored at the window open: a recompute walks only what is left of it, and it lapses 20 minutes after the open whatever is read. A verified ON on every Core of the night ends it — only when stamped at or after the window open and at or before the pool reading the plan walks from (the time and the pool are read before the recompute's first wait and the stamp after it, so a verify landing in between keeps the allowance for that one recompute, the conservative side; nothing is pushed). The plan the evening job arms from never reads it.
- **A line in (49, 50].** Judged on the unfloored walk, not on the 50% the stated level is floored at: 2026-11-27 21:30 at ×0.944 and 16 hours, a 49.99% line the median walk holds (capped at a reach of ~50.0%) and the walk from the allowance's end does not (~47.5%), is disclosed; floored, every minute would read 50% and the night be carried.
- **Which nights.** At the 4-hour cushion no line on the fixture year is above 50%, so no night changes. With more cushion hours, 57 of the fixture year's 21:30 plans at 4–20 hours on both pool bases are disclosed (45 in line mode, 12 cost-carried) on 22 dates, 53 of them one-hour windows (49 on a Friday). Replayed through the real reserve, ceiling and ON decisions, with every Core switching on and with one or two Cores 17 minutes late, at the fixture's grid and at 19.1 kW, no claimed one-hour line night closes under its line (on 1.204.0, 7 to 17 of 168 did).
- **Not covered, and not pushed:** a start worse than every Core switching on 20 minutes after the open (a ceiling re-sync beside a late Core, a re-ON that takes late), and an unpredicted load. A Core that never takes the ON is still pushed, as is a refused ceiling. After the allowance lapses, a recompute of a line in (49, 50.05] still reads the reserve-floored close. A close the walk ends on the written 50% reserve is still read at 50%, so a line in (49, 50.05] whose walk draws the pool back down to the reserve is carried (2026-10-19 21:30 on nameplate at 13 hours, a 49.63% line); a pool drawn down onto a written reserve is measured held at it on every hold under 7 hours, and three holds longer than 11.8 hours read a point under it.

### A refused arm says once that nothing is armed to carry the line

- When the arm is refused, the 21:30 notice says the add-on will not charge (or will not raise the reserve) tonight, and then appended the armed night's line note: a close above or near the line that only the unarmed charge would make (2027-04-09 at ×0.944 and 7 hours: "expected to close at ~59.5%, too close to tonight's 59.4% outage line" from a pool opening at ~51.8%). The same held for a night closing under its line, near full or above full, and the mid-day notice did the same. The caps note, every other plan's note and every armed shape are unchanged; the card already drops its line row under a refused arm.
- Under a refused arm the card's worst-case stat no longer says "reserve set to N%" (2026-10-13 at ×0.944 and 5 hours said "reserve set to 38%" while the reserve stayed at the 16% floor); it names no reserve. Armed nights keep the label.

## 1.204.0

### While the Cores are dark, the night plan takes its solar from the panel's own per-slot readings once a 7-day energy balance passes

**Night charge**

- **The change.** With the house Cores dark on the vendor cloud the Cores' own solar meter is gone, the published band has no scored day, and 1.203.0 plans every night cushion-only. The house panel still measures each slot's solar: its per-slot reading of the Core's chargers (`src{n}_pv_w`). The night plan now takes its solar from that reading, on a planning band the analytics worker builds beside the published one, when a 7-day full-day energy balance passes and every other check below holds; otherwise the night is 1.203.0's, unchanged. The reading never reaches the outage line, the runway alarm or any other alarm: a high reading can lower only the cost fill.
- **One source per slot-hour.** The slot's Core that day when its own meter covers 3,500 s of the hour; else the panel's reading when it covers 3,500 s, the energy bridge has verified the slot-day for that day's Core (its persisted trust, and at least 80% of the day's daylight windows booked as measured for that Core), neither MPPT field is frozen (the same value restated twice above 50 W) and the hour is not a daylight hour of AC charge into the Core before a night proof that the reading does not count AC as solar; else a partial Core hour; else a gap, never 0 W.
- **The 7-day energy balance.** Per slot and day, the panel's reading against what the plant's own meters account for: the slot's change of charge on the panel's own capacity, its AC out and AC in through constants measured per slot and Core against that Core's own solar meter (at least 21 whole days and 20 night hours of grid charge; 51 days a slot on the reference plant), and standing terms; a night, whose solar is 0, can only lower the estimate. It is judged as of the evening that owns the window, on whole days up to the day before: at least 7 days of panel readings in the last 14, the newest no more than two days old, every one of the last 7 weekly windows at 1.04 or less (strict; a code constant, not an option). A day counts only when every hour of every slot taking the readings resolves, under the slot-to-Core map of record, with the panel's own daily solar total matching every slot's reading summed. A night reading above 20 W, or a day whose readings miss the daily total, holds the readings off for 14 days; a reading more than 2% above a returning Core's own figure, for 30. A changed slot-to-Core map restarts the count; a swapped-in Core needs its own 21 days in the slot first.
- **Admission.** A slot-day of the panel's readings reaches the planning fit, its 7-day bias, its 30-day calibration and the ledger only when the balance judged a window holding it and none failed, each window re-judged as of the evening on what is known then. No failed window is kept durably: in a mixed state (some Cores back), a window that failed is not remembered once one of its field slots can no longer be re-judged (its constants or capacity unknown, its trust revoked, the slot removed), and the days it held of its other slots can then be admitted through later passing windows — into the planning fit, bias and calibration and the ledger (the cost fill), never the outage line.
- **The selection, per night.** The Cores' own band when it passes its gate and covers every connected slot's Core — 1.203.0's plan, byte for byte. Else the planning band, in a write mode on a fresh panel and an uncancelled night, its reports for this evening and the slots connected now, the balance passing, every connected slot resolved, a pack capacity ratio known, and the planning band passing the existing gate (at least 4 scored days, provisional below 14, coverage at least 78%). Else 1.203.0's deps exactly; a Cores' band that passed without covering every connected slot is passed as refused, so that night is 1.203.0's plan where eligible (the cushion-only plan, or before a long gap its long-gap fill); in advisory mode or on a stale panel it has no plan, as on any refused night. The mid-day, backup and morning-refresh legs, advisory mode, a stale panel and a cancelled night never take the panel's readings.
- **The evening and the latch.** The as-of day is the evening job's own day (Saturday judges as of Saturday, Sunday as of Sunday), and the evening job's own call reads no latch: Saturday's latch for the Monday window both share never decides Sunday's evening, and Sunday's plan supersedes Saturday's for that window, as before this release. Every other recompute until the window's close reads the evening's latched basis and day. A report missing inside a window latched on the panel's readings keeps the previous plan (after a restart, this window's saved plan), never a fresh selection; a latch written by 1.203.0, like that of an evening job that did not decide before its cutoff, reads as the Cores' band (1.203.0's plan while they are dark) to its close, so this release may deploy inside an armed window. Every latch this release writes carries a marker 1.203.0 never wrote, so one without it is read as written before this release. A passing reading inside such a window is refused, naming the latch's own day: "this window's evening decision was made on … without them"; where no decision was made, "no evening decision was made for this window on … (the evening job did not decide before its cutoff)"; for a latch 1.203.0 wrote, "this window's latch was written on … by a release before v1.204.0 and records no panel basis". Where an arm for the window may still hold or write and may be on the panel's readings (an earlier evening's: Saturday's, after Sunday's cutoff with no decision, or when Sunday armed nothing it delivered; or the window's own evening's on the planning band), the sentence a refused night's plan closes with, for every refusal inside that window (the 'latched' words or a refusal of the recompute's own clauses: the balance, a hold, the planning band's gate), speaks of the plan shown ("The plan shown is computed without the panel's own solar readings: …") instead of "… not used tonight", and says nothing of that arm: its state is the armed record's to show. An arm counts unless it was cancelled before anything was written, is settled as someone else's reserve, is grid-lost, or was restored with the restore read back. A plan on the panel's readings is decided at 21:30, as any solar plan is; a refused night keeps 1.203.0's 22:30 last resort. With no planning report yet after a start-up, or one built for another evening, no refusal is printed and, where the Cores' band is refused, the night waits to 22:30.
- **Words.** A plan on the readings closes its rationale with "SOLAR FROM THE PANEL'S READINGS: slots 1, 2 and 3 take their solar from the panel's own reading of each Core's chargers (their Cores did not report their own solar on …); the band is calibrated on … scored days (the panel's readings stand in on … of the last 30 days), and the 7-day energy balance reads … on the week to …: … (refused above 1.04).
