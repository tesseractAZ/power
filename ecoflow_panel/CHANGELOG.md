## 1.203.0

### While the solar basis is refused, the night plan holds the outage cushion; a reserve-held line is written one point above its whole percent

**Night charge**

- **The defect.** With the Cores dark on the vendor cloud the forecast PV is 0 and no solar calibration day scores, so every night plan was refused for its solar basis ("only 0 scored calibration day(s), need 4"), and from 2026-10-16 nothing charged overnight. The panel, its load and its reserve were reporting all along.
- **The cushion-only plan.** Where the solar basis is refused — no scored day, too few, a band-coverage miss, no widening defined for the count, or the climatology tier (a verdict on the weather, which a plan with no PV does not read) — the live overnight plan in `supervised` or `auto` mode on a fresh panel reading is sized on the outage cushion alone. It is the existing sizer with every PV figure zeroed (the next 24 h band's and the multi-day carry's P10 and P50), every solar surplus dropped, no band widening and the resilience objective, but for the long-gap fill below. It credits no solar and reads no solar calibration; the load side is unchanged. Its one test is the pack at the window close against the reserve plus the islanded-outage cushion, so it writes the line at the window open and restores the reserve at the close, as on any resilience night; above 50% the line force-charge carries it as before. It needs forecast load hours and a resolved window. No load hours, no window, `advisory` mode, a stale panel at the 22:30 last resort, the outage backup and mid-day legs, the mid-day shadow and the morning refresh are refused as before. The legacy band (no islanded-load measurement) and a disabled cushion are refused by name: "No plan — forecast/telemetry basis incomplete (… ; no cushion-only plan: no islanded-load measurement); nothing will be charged."
- **The evening.** A cushion-only plan made on a transient refusal (0 scored days, the shape a restart leaves) waits to the deferral end, as a refused plan does, so on the APS preset it is decided at 22:30 with the Cancel until the 23:00 open; on a persistent refusal it is decided at 21:30. Every recorded dark night since 2026-10-16 was already decided at 22:30.
- **Friday's long gap.** Before a gap of more than a day after its window (Friday's 23:00–00:00 window before Monday, or a holiday's own gap), a cushion-only night in cost mode keeps the cost objective with the long-gap rule and no solar headroom: the cost ceiling is `ARB_COST_MAX_SOC_PCT`, and the cost force-charge rides it to its stop through the existing just-in-time start, stop and window-close OFF. It needs `ARB_OBJECTIVE` `cost` and `supervised` or `auto`; the cost force-charge carries it above the 50% reserve only with `ARB_COST_MAX_SOC_PCT` above 50 (at 50 or under, the reserve write alone fills to that ceiling). **In resilience mode no fill runs**: the night holds the outage line at its close only, and the plan says so. The gap is the one after the night's own window, so Thursday's 6 h window (Friday's window opens 18 h after it closes) gets neither the fill nor the gap clause. The option texts for `ARB_OBJECTIVE` and `ARB_COST_MAX_SOC_PCT`, in English, in Spanish and in the options guide, say that while the solar basis is refused only this night fills and Thursday holds the outage cushion only.
- **The pool.** The night-charge cushion tracker was added in 1.199.0, after the Cores went dark, and had never seen a capacity ratio, so the plan counted the pool on nameplate while the runway counted it on the Cores' held ratios. It is now seeded, read-only, from the runway's tracker file while it has no ratio of its own: the per-Core ratios with their own timestamps, never the runway's applied panel figures, so the plan's scale is the runway's at every instant — held, ×0.944 on the reference plant's 0.940/0.919/0.973, until those ratios turn 30 days old at about 2026-11-14 01:54 MST, then the lowest known, ×0.919, until every connected Core reports complete packs again. With no ratio known at all, a cushion-only plan counts its pool on `CAPACITY_SCALE_MIN` (0.8 unless set) and says so; a smaller pool is a higher line.

The point is measured at a 50% reserve only; no charge-up to a lower reserve is on record.

## 1.202.0

### The night-charge plan walks on the packs' measured capacity and drains the Cores' standby

**Night charge**

- Since 1.199.0 the outage-cushion line has been a percent of the packs' measured capacity (their BMS full-charge capacity, about 0.944 of nameplate on the reference plant), the basis of the reserve percent the panel holds. The walks that size the night still counted nameplate kWh per percent and drained the panel load ÷ the discharge efficiency with no standby; since 1.201.0 the cushion counts the Cores' standby for its hours, but the walks to the window close did not. On the reference plant a point of the pool held 0.870 kWh in the line and 0.922 kWh in the walks, and every hour before the close left out about 270 Wh of drain.
- **The fix.** The plan counts its pool once, after the refusals: the panel's nameplate figure × the measured capacity scale (`plannerPoolKwh`). Every walk, clamp and kWh per percent follows from it, and the line is the reserve plus the cushion on it, the same percent as 1.201.0's. The pool rides the scale on every basis the capacity tracker reports a plausible ratio on (`measured`, `held`, `min-known`). With no scale, a scale outside 0.5–1 or a scale of 1 the pool is the panel's figure.
- **The walks drain the Cores' standby.** The figure is 1.201.0's (the runway's for the connected Cores, read once by the planner; 0 W with none or with no reading). It is drained pack-side on every discharge leg: the islanded walks to the close, the share of each window hour the grid is not charging, the expected carry, the walk to a just-in-time start, and a line walk before its start and while latched. It is never drained from a charging gain (the grid feeds the Cores while it charges them), from the morning surplus, or in the cushion's hours, which start at the close where the walks end. Where a figure above 0 W is drained, a night the window cannot meet is sized at the window's full effort, as 1.201.0 does for the cushion's standby.
- **What keeps the panel's figure.** The live force-charge times its start on the panel's capacity figure, so the plan's mirror of it does too: the stated start is the minute that tick comes due along the plan's own walk. The cost room's conversion is 1.201.0's: the morning surplus is counted × pool ÷ panel in the cost ceiling, the cost target and the over-buy flag, and the de-bias bound is on the panel's figure, because the walks now drain the standby and the surplus does not subtract it. The headroom it converts can move, since the de-bias reads the walks twice: it applies only on a night whose walks hold its line (its gate), and it never takes the ceiling under the pool level the resilience buy lifts to on those walks (its bound). On a scale of 1, 49 ceilings and 22 stops rise and none falls. Every fall is the gate (below, "The de-bias's gate"). The reference plant's two cost nights keep their ceilings, stops, writes and starts.
