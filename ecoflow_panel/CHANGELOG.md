## 1.200.1

### A line night rides a reserve that already reads 50%, with no reserve write and no restore

**Night charge**

- In 1.200.0 the force-charge for a line above the panel's 50% reserve rode a verified reserve write, so a panel already reading 50% armed nothing: no write was due, none verified, and the night was disclosed (`reserveAtTarget`). On a plant whose reserve stands at 50 that was every charge night with a line above it. Now the plan keeps the ask whenever the panel reads the write, and the plan-time reading decides words only (`lineReserveStands`). A reading above the write, and a mid-day leg at it, stay disclosed.
- **The stand.** A record with a line ask that reads the target, strictly, on a tick inside the apply window stamps `reserveStandingAtMs`. The stamp sits behind every apply guard: the mode, a cancel, the on-peak guard, vitals, coherence and the envelope, and it needs no attempt on record, a grid not read lost, and no write block. It writes nothing. `appliedAtMs`, `applyVerifiedAtMs` and `priorReservePct` stay null, so no restore can go, and the reserve stays the panel's own.
- **The ride.** The ceiling sync and the ON need the stamp and a fresh reading of the target on the same tick (`reserveRideReady`, `ForceChargeOpts.reservePct`). A reading that moved or went missing declines the start and, if it holds 10 minutes, pushes. The OFF side, the wall-clock deadline and the ceiling's return read no write, and are unchanged.
- **A person's reserve wins.** After the stamp no apply can follow, so nothing raises a reading a person lowered. The foreign rule runs to the close on two equal readings 5 minutes apart, and ends the ride for the night, writing nothing. The add-on's own reserve floor route lands there too, so those words say the reserve was changed and not who changed it.
- **A grid loss is one-way.** `gridPresent` false on the tick that would stamp, or on a stood night at any tick before its close, stamps `gridLostAtMs`, once, with no dwell: no stamp, no apply and no start after it, whatever the grid or the reading does next, Charge Now off if it is on, the panel's ceiling back at once if it was moved, and a push says what can still lift the pool. On a record with no stamp that is a ride lost, not a write missed: no missed push, a leg that reads `completed`, and the close's account says the grid was lost. A not-OK `gridSta` with no live grid import reads the grid lost; beside live import it reads the grid present, holds the ON, switches a run off, and stamps no `gridLostAtMs`. A grid unknown stamps, and the ON needs it known. Where the reserve reads the target with every other check clear, a grid the panel still reads lost at the open loses the ride, whenever that reading was taken; a tick another apply guard refused decides nothing, so a grid read lost only on such ticks leaves no stamp of either kind.
- **A night lowered after the plan.** A reserve of 50 at the plan and under it at the open keeps its ask, gets its write and rides it. A night raised to 50 after the plan rides the stand. Before, the first had no line words and the second ended in "no reserve write was confirmed".
- **The close.** No restore is due. The account is the morning summary, with the panel's stop, the re-ONs and the close against the line as on a written night, and "the reserve stood at 50%" in place of the raise and the restore. A leg reads `completed` once it finishes, with no Cancel. The house-panel pin waits for the close, since no reserve write holds it on such a night.
- **Ledger and readiness.** A stood night stamps a `reserve stood — ` disposition and leaves `actuated` unset, so the gate grades it (a claimed night that closes under is a strike, and an armed one that never ran and closed under) while it counts toward no graduation and no streak, and clears no strike. A grid loss at the stamp stamps the same prefix, so the gate grades it as a stood night that lost the grid, with no exemption for the grid on either. A cancel before any ON rewrites the disposition, so a person's cancel is no strike. `delivered_kwh` is measured on such a row, and the buy de-bias stays off it.
- **A write verified after `applyFailed`.** The ledger stamped `actuated` 0 and nothing moved it back, so the gate missed a strike on a real night. A verify or a ceiling after the escalation now stamps `actuated` 1. A normal night writes nothing new.
- **Words.** The evening push says no write is due while the reserve reads 50%, and that no restore is due then; a reading under 50% at the open lets the armed write go, the force-charge rides it, and that reading is restored after the window. The sentence "before at 11:00 PM" in the reserve-at-target Cancel line now reads "before the window opens". The missed-apply reason on a line ask names the write block first, and otherwise that the reserve reading did not reach the add-on with every check clear while the apply window was open. The card's footer says a write goes only when the reserve reads under the night's target, and that a reserve already reading it gets no write and no restore; its reserve figure reads "reserve reads 50%" on a plan whose reserve stands; and its no-plan line says the night armed on the standing reserve can be cancelled.
- **What a force-charge rides and what stops it.** SECURITY.md, DOCS.md, the inventory and chapter 15 said a force-charge runs only on a verified reserve write, and two of them named cost options as stopping every one. A line night also rides a reserve that already read the target behind the same guards. `ARB_COST_MAX_SOC_PCT` 50 does not stop the line force-charge; `NIGHT_CHARGE_MODE` `advisory` or the night's Cancel does. `ARB_OBJECTIVE` `resilience` and `ARB_COST_MAX_SOC_PCT` 50 stop the cost force-charge alone. A line night's start can come early in the window.

**What is disclosed**

- **Charge Now's exposure.** On a plant whose reserve stands at 50, every charge night with a line ask rides. Emulated over the year's nights, Charge Now reads ON a mean 116-176 minutes a night across 4-20 h of cushion, against 0 before (p95 232-296). How a slot with Charge Now on behaves when the grid fails is unproven: the attended test has not been run.
- **The lowered night's ask.** It rides the ask sized on a floor of 50, as announced. That closes a mean 28 points above the line the lowered reserve would ask, and buys about 18 kWh more than before (51.9 at most). On 4,641 of 8,561 walked cells the lowered reserve would have asked no line at all. It fails high and costs money, not safety. Such a night can be struck at a line its reserve never asked; there is no exemption.
- **Not identical on a cost night with an ask.** A cost-dominant night with a line ask at a standing 50 now gets an ON in cost mode (1,008 of 8,561 walked cells). A cost night with no ask is byte-identical.
- **The re-entry band.** After the pool stops at a reserve the panel resumes discharging only some 20 points above it, measured against 16%. At a reserve of 50 that is unmeasured. A stood night that closes under about 70-75% may sit in it and buy through on-peak: 1,938 of 8,561 walked cells closed under 75%, and the walk does not model the band. The words no longer say the house uses the pool as usual, and no hold rule is built on an unmeasured band.
- **No graduation evidence.** A plant that only stands stays `LEARNING`, and a strike there ages out rather than clearing.
- **No proof of the write path on the night.** The ceiling sync or the ON may be such a night's first device write. Their own readback, re-issue and failure push cover it.
- **Nothing here has run on the plant.** Every figure is emulated: 92.16 × 0.944 kWh, hourly P50 loads, the panel's measured OFF at its ceiling, 60 s ticks. The ON-reading latch is not modelled in that walk. The live plant's line is 31-33% and its plans are blocked, so its writes are unchanged, and the first real run may be months off.

**Tests**

- `lineStandingReserve.test.ts` pins the plan, the stamp, the ride, each record reader, the OFF and the ceiling, the texts, the gate and the wiring. `lineStandingWalk.test.ts` walks the year through the real decisions: reserve writes 0 and restores 0 on 8,561 standing cells, ON on 8,561 against 0, a mean close of 83.4 against 52.9, the ceiling back on every one, and 188 claimed nights under against 184 on the written control. With the grid an input to that walk, no stood night rides after a blip of 1 to 30 minutes from the open. A digest of 20,030 no-move walks, generated on 1.200.0, holds their writes byte for byte. `mutate-line-standing.mjs` restores each defect, the ones the review reproduced included.
- T7's plant gained a blocker that ends, so its grids walk the add-on's own start, not a Charge Now re-asserted every tick. Every pin held.
